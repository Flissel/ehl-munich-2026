  ### 2. Auth for humans (the crux)
  - **Harbor is only a resource server, not an OAuth server** (`AGENTS.md` L112, `auth-oidc.ts` L9-15). It verifies JWTs from one pinned `AUTH_ISSUER`:

  - The spec says you paste the org address, then do OAuth via RFC 9728 → AS discovery, and tokens go to the keychain (`SPEC.md` L144).
