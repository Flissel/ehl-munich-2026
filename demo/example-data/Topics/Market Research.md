## Key facts
- BMW Group deliveries 2025: 2,463,715 (+0.5 %); BMW brand 2,169,761 (-1.4 %), MINI 288,290 (+17.7 %) [source](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0454859EN/bmw-group-vehicle-sales-grew-in-2025-)
- Revenues 2025: EUR 133,453 m (-6.3 %); Automotive EBIT margin 5.3 % (2024: 6.3 %); outlook 2026: 4 to 6 % [source](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0456175EN/stable-group-earnings-thanks-to-consistent-strategy:-bmw-group-on-track)
- R&D expenditure 2025: EUR 8,319 m, R&D ratio 6.2 %; capex EUR 7,237 m [source](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0456175EN/stable-group-earnings-thanks-to-consistent-strategy:-bmw-group-on-track)
- H1 2026 deliveries: 1,156,742 (-4.2 %); BMW brand 1,004,681 (-6.2 %) [source](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0459223EN/)
- Dataset/: customer feedback F70, G60, G70 (sources A to D), customer studies 2025 (China, EU, US), sales volumes 2024, 2025 and 2030 plan, German price lists July 2026
- Customer themes come from a hand written lookup of BMW component tags (demo/data/themes.csv); study items are mapped the same way (demo/data/study_themes.csv)
