### F70-0817

- Source: Source A
- Vehicle: F70
- Market: JP
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect, Difficult to Use, Likes, unknown_feedback_type
- Signal types: complaint, praise, unknown

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Air conditioning, setting : Malfunction / Air conditioning, setting / Malfunction
- Likes (praise): ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology : Like: function / ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology / Like: function
- Difficult to Use (complaint): ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology : Not user-friendly / ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology / Not user-friendly
- Likes (praise): Drivetrain : Like: performance / Drivetrain / Like: performance
- Defect (complaint): Touch screen, operation : Malfunction / Touch screen, operation / Malfunction
- Defect (complaint): Transmission : Malfunction / Transmission / Malfunction
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Voice control / speech recognition : Not user-friendly / Voice control / speech recognition / Not user-friendly
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> i am happy with how the car performs and with the driving itself, but i feel the control system and software still need work. in particular, these areas are below what i could recommend.

[controls and ui]
- removing the physical buttons and idrive dial has made operation while driving much worse.
- the flat panel buttons are hard to find by feel, and the whole panel sinking when pressed plus the reaction lag hurts usability.
- the ui behavior is odd, not returning to the home screen after changing modes, leaving the selection screen up.
- using the air conditioner takes several taps, which increases workload and harms safety while driving.

[driving behavior]
- from a standstill, there are times when the car behaves unnaturally, with a brief torque drop as it starts to accelerate (maybe related to auto hold release timing, mild aeb action, or dct clutch control).

[voice control]
- responses are delayed and often misrecognized, and above all, with several occupants i do not want to use voice commands, so it is not practical.
- the voice-centered ui design has not actually made operation easier.

while the vehicle itself is very appealing, the current state of the operation system and software makes it hard to strongly recommend. i hope future software updates will improve this.
