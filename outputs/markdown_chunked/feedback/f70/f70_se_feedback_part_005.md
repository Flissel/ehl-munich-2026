- Defect (complaint): Instrument panel : Damage / Instrument panel / Damage
- Likes (praise): Touch screen, operation : Like: usability / Touch screen, operation / Like: usability
- Wants (unmet_need): Wheels / tires : Customer request: design / Wheels / tires / Customer request: design
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> overall the car is good, but my score drops because of a few things:
* the xdrive feels too front-wheel biased. a rear-wheel bias would have suited it better and been more fun, especially since this is supposed to be a semi-enthusiast car with 300hp and a price around 500 tkr.
* there are shift paddles instead of a proper gear lever. even a sequential "fake" shift lever for the dct would have felt better than paddles. the paddles come across as a gimmick and really only make sense on the highway. when you are turning or trying to drift, the paddles are basically useless.
* there are no physical controls for climate comfort like ac or seat heating. the screen is extremely clunky for the things you change all the time, such as temperature. for everything else, the screen works great.
* the software is buggy. sometimes the infotainment and the dashboard freeze and i have to restart them by pressing and holding the volume scroll buton.

i honestly wouldnt have gone for this car if it werent for the excellent leasing offer, about 4500 sek per month for 2 years. the choice was between leasing this or buying an old tuned volvo 740. i am happy i picked this one, but when you want to have fun driving, the gear paddles and the slightly too strong front-wheel bias do make it feel a bit limited.
