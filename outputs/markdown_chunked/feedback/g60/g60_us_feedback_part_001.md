### G60-0011

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote controlled parking : Not user-friendly / Remote controlled parking / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer wanted to know whether his car includes the remote control parking feature and, if it does, how to operate it. Writer verified that the vehicle is indeed equipped with remote control parking. Writer then called the customer back and walked him through the steps for using it. Writer first confirmed the customer already has the digital key plus configured on his device, and customer answered yes. Writer advised that he should begin the automatic parking process as he normally does by pressing the P button at low speed to bring up the parking cameras, which customer acknowledged. Writer then explained that once parking is activated, he must press the brake, set the parking brake, and follow the on-screen prompt to continue with the maneuver assistant. Writer instructed that after choosing that option, he should exit the car and open the My BMW App, select the remote control parking tile on the main screen, and then follow the instructions to move the phone forward and backward to align with the two targets. Writer added that once that is done, he needs to press and hold the blue circle in the app to finish the parking process. Customer stated he understood and said he would test it out.
