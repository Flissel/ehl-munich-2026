### G60-0024

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Digital Key : Not user-friendly / Digital Key / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the dealer representative told them they would not need the key fob once the phone was set up with the app and digital key. Customer explained that on one occasion they went to the garage with the key fob left upstairs and were unable to unlock the car using the app. Customer expressed concern about potentially being stuck in a shopping center parking lot if they relied on not bringing the key fob and then could not unlock the vehicle. Customer stated the digital key works inconsistently and, although it has worked at times, they expect it to function every time. Customer mentioned they still carry the key fob because they do not trust the digital key fully, and that the wallet version of the key is less reliable than the app. Writer explained that Samsung Wallet or Apple Wallet may require an authentication step before the key is active, which can lead to inconsistent operation. Writer recommended using the key fob as a backup. Writer explained that if the vehicle is in sleep or standby mode, it may need to be awakened by holding the phone near the door handle. Writer acknowledged the customer wants the digital key to behave like the key fob, unlocking on approach and locking when walking away with complete reliability. Writer confirmed this feedback would be documented.
