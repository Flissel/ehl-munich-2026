### G60-0029

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Interior rearview mirror : Not user-friendly / Interior rearview mirror / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer inquired about how to adjust the interior rearview mirror. Customer said she saw some buttons underneath the mirror and was not sure if those were used to change its position. Writer explained that the buttons are for the universal garage door opener and can be programmed with a handheld transmitter, and that the mirror itself is adjusted manually. Customer replied that this made sense and added that the mirror felt quite stiff, so she wanted to confirm before trying to move it. Writer stated that this stiffness sounds normal and that using a bit more force should let her set it where she wants. Customer thanked the writer.
