Customer comment:

> Customer stated that at times when he gets out of the car the radio keeps playing. Writer advised this can occur if it is still connected to his phone, then walked him through iDrive to apps, vehicle, doors and windows, lock and unlock, to choose the option for the car to power down after the door is opened.
