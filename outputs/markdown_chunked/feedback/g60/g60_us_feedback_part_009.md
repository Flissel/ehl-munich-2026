### G60-0126

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Charge high-voltage battery : Not user-friendly / Charge high-voltage battery / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported the vehicle has inconsistent scheduled charging, with the car often ignoring the programmed 8:30 PM time slot. Customer noted that sometimes it begins charging immediately after being plugged in even though the settings are not set to charge right away, and at other times it will unexpectedly start charging around 5 PM or 4 PM despite being set for 8:30 PM. Customer stated the system will also change the charging configuration on its own, switching from the time slot setting to charge immediately without any action from the customer. Customer estimated this behavior occurs about half the time, with the other 50 percent working as intended. Customer indicated the issue happens whether charging from roughly 75 percent state of charge to a 90 percent target or at other battery levels, and that it was present both before and after a software update. Customer stated all charging is done at home using a 250 volt outlet in the garage. Customer mentioned the AC limit was originally at 48 amps but later reduced to 32 amps to be cautious, and the problem occurs at both limits. Customer added the outlet is capable of supporting up to 48 amps. Customer advised the vehicle was brought to the dealership, but technicians were unable to duplicate the problem even though the customer provided app screenshots showing the time slot at 8:30 PM while the car charged at 8 PM. Customer said the dealership performed tests and reached out to BMW engineers, but they were unable to resolve the issue. Customer stated the dealership did not reset the software or apply any fix, and engineers concluded there was nothing to be done. Writer suggested the customer continue to observe the charging behavior and contact the dealership again if the problem persists so they can try to reproduce and diagnose it.
