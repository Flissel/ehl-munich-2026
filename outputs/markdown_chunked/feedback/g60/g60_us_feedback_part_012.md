### G60-0164

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Starting engine : Malfunction / Starting engine / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated her vehicle began acting weird today. Customer stated she is unsure what is going on, but the vehicle will no longer detect her digital key on her phone or her key fob. Customer stated the only way she has managed to start the vehicle is by placing the digital key card in the phone tray. Customer stated she has also had to unlock the vehicle manually, either using the app or by pressing a button on the key fob. Customer stated comfort access, including unlock on approach and lock when walking away, is not working. Customer stated that when she gets in, the vehicle displays that the key is not detected and instructs her to place the key in the phone tray, which does allow the vehicle to start. Writer had her perform a 30 second reboot and then walk away from the vehicle to verify the doors would lock, but Customer stated the doors did not lock automatically as she walked away. Writer then had her perform a 70 second system reboot. Customer stated the vehicle reported that it had updated features and asked her to check privacy data. Writer advised her to go to Apps>System settings>data privacy and review what was updated. Customer stated she selected all the options there. Writer then used RVG, guiding her to apps and services and having her update the apps and services. Writer next guided her to Remote software update to confirm the software version, which was 03/2024.40, and had her check for further updates, but none were available. Writer advised her to go to BMW ID>Settings>driver recognition, where Writer discovered that the digital key on her phone was not being recognized, while the key fob was. Customer asked if she should try walking away and returning with only the phone, and Writer advised her to test that. Customer stated that as she walked away, the vehicle locked like it had previously, and when she approached with just the digital key on her phone, the doors unlocked, her driver profile loaded, and she was able to start the vehicle. Customer thanked the Writer for his help.
