# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0293

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Door, open / close : Not user-friendly / Door, open / close / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer asked whether the i5 doors use a cable-driven door pull so you can get out if the battery dies. Writer advised that when the vehicle is stopped you should be able to pull the door handle and the door should open. Customer stated a friend had warned him about an issue with Tesla vehicles that use an electronic door opening system where you must know where the emergency release is. Customer added he heard a story about someone being trapped inside their Tesla because of that electronic door opening system. Writer advised he would look into it further and get back to the customer.
Update 3/03/25:
Writer determined the door levers on this vehicle are mechanical and do not require an emergency release lever like Tesla. Writer called the customer back and advised the doors and their levers are mechanical.
