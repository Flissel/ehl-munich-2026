### G60-0307

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): My Modes / driving experience : Not user-friendly / My Modes / driving experience / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer was unsure how the various modes operate and what their purposes are. Writer explained that personal mode functions as the standard mode and is the one that provides the highest level of customization for the vehicle settings. Writer further noted that sport mode, which the customer already knew about, is geared toward delivering increased performance response. Writer then clarified that efficient mode is essentially the previous eco pro mode, intended to be the most economy focused. Writer added that the other available modes mainly adjust styling elements and are primarily aesthetic or design related.
