Classifications:

- Likes (praise): Body equipment, overall technology : Like: price-performance ratio / Body equipment, overall technology / Like: price-performance ratio
- Likes (praise): Drive, driving dynamics and chassis, overall technology : Like: function / Drive, driving dynamics and chassis, overall technology / Like: function
- Defect (complaint): Seats : Damage / Seats / Damage
- Likes (praise): Seats : Like: function / Seats / Like: function
- Likes (praise): Seats : Likes comfort / Seats / Likes comfort
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> this car ticks almost all the boxes for me. the key thing is that it is genuinely fun to drive. on top of that, the purchase price felt competitive and the overall value for a new car seemed good.

the downside is the complexity of the technology. it is not straightforward to learn, and making it simpler would be a very good objective.

the big negative so far has been getting the driver seat adjusted so i do not end up with serious pain in my lower right back and right knee. i spent hours trying to find a position that worked. my son had to set the seat for me again this week, the second time, and now i can drive for about 30 minutes without pain. i am hoping that will also hold up for my regular 2 hour trips. over the last roughly 35 years i have had teslas and e class mercedes and never ran into this problem. my last mercedes had multi contour seats. my i5 has the upgraded seat package, but i just cannot get it dialed in, there are simply too many adjustments. the current e class mercedes offers an electronic assistant to help the driver find a seat position, and maybe bmw should add something like that too.
