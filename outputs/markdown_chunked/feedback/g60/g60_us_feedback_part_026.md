### G60-0349

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Speed Limit Assistant : Not user-friendly / Speed Limit Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated by email that on many streets near him the speed limit signs sit roughly 20 feet off the main roadway because of the bicycle lane and parked cars, and that the Speed Limit Assistant almost never reads those signs or changes the Speed Limit display properly. Customer stated his prior Lexus was much better at recognizing those kinds of signs. Customer further stated that when he drives past a School Speed Limit sign that only lowers the limit during school hours when the lights are flashing, the Speed Limit Assistant still shows the reduced speed even if the restriction does not actually apply. Writer advised the customer about the feature's limitations with geocoded data and how the front camera is used to detect traffic signs.
