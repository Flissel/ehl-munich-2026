### G60-0398

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Electric Range : Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range) / Electric Range / Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range)

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is worried about how the range is estimated. Customer stated he drove a 100 mile round trip into the city and back home. Customer stated the car showed roughly 200 miles of range at departure and less than 60 miles remaining once he got back. Customer stated he used efficient mode only for part of the drive home because he began to doubt if there was enough charge to reach his house. Customer stated the average outside temperature was about 32-35 degrees. Customer stated he did not precondition the vehicle before leaving, but the car was kept in his garage overnight. Customer stated the dealership did not clearly explain how to get the best range, and that this is his first electric vehicle.

Writer advised the figure in the instrument cluster is only an estimate. Writer advised that range is affected by climate control usage, driving style, charging behavior, weather, road terrain, and some other variables. Writer advised that preconditioning the vehicle on a cold day will provide a much more accurate range prediction. Writer advised that heating the vehicle up to optimal temperature consumes a lot of battery power, and that not just the cabin but also the battery itself must be warmed to operate efficiently.
