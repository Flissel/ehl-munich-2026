# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0403

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Braking : Noise / Braking / Noise

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that the vehicle has been producing a squeaking sound from the brakes after only about four weeks of ownership, and that the issue happens whenever the brake pedal is pressed. Customer stated the noise is loud and ongoing and that it makes the car unpleasant to drive. Customer emphasized that this does not seem like a normal brake sound and expressed concern about what might be causing it. Customer noted that when the car was kept overnight at the dealership and a loaner of the same model was provided, the brakes on the loaner worked fine and did not make any noise. Customer stated the dealership has not been able to identify the source of the problem even after inspecting the vehicle, and believes the brake pads should be removed and examined properly. Customer mentioned this is the fourth BMW they have owned and that none of the prior vehicles had similar issues. Customer expressed strong dissatisfaction, saying the dealership is not performing the necessary diagnostic work. Writer suggested taking the car to another dealership for a second opinion if the customer cannot reach an understanding with the current service manager. Writer also advised that high performance brakes can produce noise.
