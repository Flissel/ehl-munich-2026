### G60-0444

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Charge high-voltage battery : Not user-friendly / Charge high-voltage battery / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer mentioned seeing guidance to only charge the battery to 80% and asked if that was accurate and whether he could still charge to 100%. Writer explained that routinely charging to around 80% is recommended, since the energy above that tends to run down faster, the car charges more slowly, and it helps the battery over the long term. Writer also advised that charging to 100% from time to time is acceptable when extra range is needed for a trip or similar situations.
