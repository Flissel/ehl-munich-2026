# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0499

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Ambient light : Customer request: usability / Ambient light / Customer request: usability

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that red is missing from the ambient lighting color choices and expressed confusion about why both red and blue are not provided when the M package is selected, since red and blue are the recognizable signature colors of M. Customer stated they looked online and found many other owners also puzzled by this restriction. Customer stated BMW previously offered red as an option until roughly a year or two ago, when it was either removed through an over-the-air update or when the G60 model was introduced. Customer stated the current palette includes coral and pearl white and asked who would actually choose pearl white inside a car, stating these colors do not fit the character of M mode. Customer stated that when using M mode, drivers expect red and blue lighting, not coral and blue.
