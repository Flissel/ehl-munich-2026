### G60-0560

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, lock / unlock : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, lock / unlock / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated she is still trying to figure out the right way to unlock the doors, and she is usually alone when she needs to get to the back of the car. Customer stated she thought she had turned off the automatic door lock because she wants the rear door to unlock at the same time, so she does not have to lean back inside to open it. Customer stated she believed the dealer had switched that off and that when she gets into the car it locks all the doors and then unlocks them all, which is what she was looking for. Customer stated the dealer explained that for safety the back door is normally kept locked, and he did disable that for her, but when she gets out of the car and opens the doors she expects all of them to unlock again, and they do not, so she has to unlock them manually. Writer explained that the setting has to be checked in the menu and that the customer can choose whether the doors unlock when arriving at the destination or when in park. Writer asked if the customer was able to go to the car right then, but Customer stated she was in the middle of a tesing site with a friend doing some things and could not get to the vehicle. Writer advised Customer to go into Doors and Windows when she has a moment and check what she has selected, as it will show all the different options.
