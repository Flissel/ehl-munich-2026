# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0643

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Not comfortable / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Not comfortable

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that if he had the choice, he would change how the auto start-stop works. Customer commented that the system does not feel as smooth as he would like when pulling away, and that he notices the way it turns off and back on. Writer explained that besides turning off the MSA for that drive, there is also an Auto H button located near the start-stop button that the customer should try the next time he is in the car. Writer went over the Auto H, or auto hold, function and described how, when the vehicle is brought to a stop, it will remain stopped even after the driver lifts their foot off the brake pedal, and will only move once the accelerator pedal is pressed. Writer gave the example of sitting at a red light and asked the customer to see whether the car stays stationary or begins to roll ahead when the brake pedal is released before pressing the accelerator. Writer instructed the customer to check for a green light on the Auto H button and stated that a quick press will switch the feature off. Writer clarified that this feature is very useful in stop-and-go traffic, drive-throughs and similar situations where the driver wants to take their foot off the brake while the vehicle remains stopped until the accelerator is used. Writer also noted that auto hold can sometimes lead to slightly jerky movements when parking and suggested this could be playing a role in the smoothness concern the customer mentioned.

### G60-0647

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): (Active) cruise control : Not user-friendly / (Active) cruise control / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he liked how the cruise distance control worked on the i4 better. Customer stated the i4 had a toggle on the left side of the steering wheel that let him change the distance much faster and more easily. Customer stated that on the i5 he has to go into a menu to adjust it. Customer stated it feels like BMW has gone to more of a set and forget system. Customer stated he prefers to adjust the distance based on the trip or driving conditions, and having the controls on the steering wheel made changing the distance much easier while driving.

### G60-0648

- Source: Source B

- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Wants

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Instrument cluster : Customer request: function / Instrument cluster / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when he is driving, he typically sees a very nice screen in the instrument cluster that shows how efficiently he is driving and how much range he is gaining through regenerative braking. Customer stated he has not been able to locate this particular view on his own by going through the different display layouts and content options. Writer asked where he sees this view appear. Customer acknowledged.
