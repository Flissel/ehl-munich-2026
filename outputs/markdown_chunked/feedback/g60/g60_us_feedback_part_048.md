### G60-0660

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Wi-Fi connection : Connection fault / Wi-Fi connection / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that the vehicle's internet connection has been unreliable ever since he took ownership. Writer asked if he currently has an active plan, and customer confirmed that he does. Customer explained that the issue is constant, sometimes the internet will come on 10 to 15 minutes after starting the car and other times it will not come on at all. Customer stated this does not depend on where he is and that it happens right when the vehicle is first started. Writer inquired if there was any pattern or if it happened only in poor coverage areas, and customer reiterated that it is not tied to specific locations but to startup. Writer went over a troubleshooting step, advising the customer to perform a hard reboot by holding the volume button for about 70 seconds until the screen turns off and comes back on three times. Writer noted this would not erase any of his settings and likened it to rebooting a phone rather than doing a factory reset.
