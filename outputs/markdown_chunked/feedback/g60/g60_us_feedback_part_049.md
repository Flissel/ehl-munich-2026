### G60-0669

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Speed Limit Assistant : Not user-friendly / Speed Limit Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when he attempts to set the cruise control, the system usually ends up roughly 5 mph faster than what he selected. Customer stated that if he chooses 50 mph, it will often jump up to around 55. Writer asked if the posted speed limit there is 50 or 55 mph. Customer stated it is probably 55 and he is trying to cruise slightly under that. Writer advised the Customer to look at the settings for the speed limit assistant, as pressing the SET button may be using the speed the car reads on the road instead of the speed the Customer is trying to input.
