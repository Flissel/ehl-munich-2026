### G60-0739

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Comfort Access : Customer request: function / Comfort Access / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer explained that on her 2022 model she was able to lock the car and, when approaching it, she could tap the left rear door and all of the doors would unlock, but that this function no longer works on the current vehicle. Customer stated that now, when she gets close and opens the front passenger side door, all doors then open, and asked if there is any way to program it so she could touch the rear passenger door and have all doors unlock. Writer advised that on this model year the rear doors do not have comfort access and that the feature is only present on the two front doors, which is why the vehicle is operating this way. Customer thanked the writer for clarifying the issue.

> Customer stated that after turning the engine off and walking away, his phone would remain connected to the vehicle and he could not use siri normally.
