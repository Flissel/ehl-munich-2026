# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0765

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Driver profile : Customer request: function / Driver profile / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer mentioned she would like to have a way to change the name that appears when the vehicle starts. She explained that, as the purchaser, her full first name is displayed on the screen at startup, and she would rather have the option to show a nickname for privacy when other people are in the car. Writer clarified that the primary driver profile must remain linked to the warranty and therefore must use the legal name, but suggested she could set up a second driver profile under a nickname and use that as her regular profile to address the privacy concern. Customer replied that it is not a major issue because not many people ride with her, and she does not want to go out of her way to create an additional profile. Writer recognized the feedback and stated he would note her preference to be able to enter a nickname in the profile.

### G60-0769

- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Electric Range : Range, not OK / Electric Range / Range, not OK

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the range feels a bit limited and the battery appears to deplete faster than he expected. Customer explained the car might show 240 miles at full charge, but after driving roughly a mile it can drop to 235. Customer noted he could likely stretch the range by driving very carefully and conservatively, but since it is a performance car he prefers to use it that way. Writer asked if he routinely charges the vehicle to full capacity. Customer confirmed he does, and writer advised that for long-term battery health he should normally charge only to 80% unless he specifically needs the entire battery.
