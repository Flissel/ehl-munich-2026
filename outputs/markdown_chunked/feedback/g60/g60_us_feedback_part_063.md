> even though i love how the car handles, there are a few things you took away that my 2021 had and i just don't get why. you removed the pockets on the back of the seats. maybe that cut production costs, but without those pockets i have to dump stuff like umbrellas and the kids' things on the rear floor. the worst part, and honestly a deal breaker when my lease is up, is the trunk size. i can't fit my grandchild's stroller in there, or a full food shop, or my mother's walker. i also dislike the silver/chrome 5s on the rear windows, it's a luxury vehicle and i don't need it shouting that it's a 5 series. the glove box is smaller now, same with the door wells, and i really don't like that i can't leave anything in the console tray since i'm no longer able to cover it. i'm forced to look away from the road to manage the controls because the knobs are gone and everything is flat. with the fan vents hidden and streamlined, i can't adjust the airflow direction anymore. i could keep going, but i'm sure the team who came up with this very modern car is pleased with what they made. for me, if these things aren't changed by the time my lease ends, i'll most likely move to another luxury brand. i genuinely hope you take this feedback on board. when i checked reviews afterwards, i saw these issues weren't just mine, a lot of people felt the same way. i really believe i'm exactly the kind of customer you target, so i hope you treat my comments seriously. my whole family drives bmws and nobody from about 35 to 70 is happy with the new design. it feels very clunky and not sleek anymore. i've already told my sales person all of this. again, i hope you truly take in and value my feedback.
