# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-0885

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Seats : Noise / Seats / Noise

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has owned BMWs for many years and this has been a recurring issue on all his prior vehicles, including several 7 series and 5 series models. Customer explained that when there is no passenger in the front seat, the front headrest rattles whenever driving over a bump or pothole. Customer confirmed he picked up on this right after purchasing the car, but remains convinced it is normal behavior. Customer mentioned he plans to bring it up with the dealership when he goes in for routine maintenance. Customer added that others have commented on the same thing and asked whether this is something we have heard about. Writer responded that nothing should be making noise in that situation and agreed it should be discussed with the dealership. Customer remarked that it feels slightly worse than on his previous vehicle, but he suspects he is just noticing it more because the car is usually operating in electric mode.

### G60-0891

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Resting state after door opening : Not user-friendly / Resting state after door opening / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer mentioned she feels like she must be doing something wrong whenever she gets out of the car. Customer said that if she has a passenger and wants to keep the vehicle running for their comfort, the car switches itself off anyway. Customer said she plans to go back to her Genius to learn how to stop this from happening. Writer explained she is not doing anything incorrectly. Writer explained this is a built-in safety function. Writer noted that when the driver unbuckles the seat belt and opens the driver door, the vehicle will power down automatically. Writer noted this is intended to stop the car from rolling over someone and also works as an anti-theft measure. Writer advised that if she wants to leave someone inside and keep them comfortable, she should activate preconditioning when she exits, which keeps the cabin comfortable for around 15 minutes. Writer clarified that preconditioning keeps the interior pleasant but does not leave the vehicle in a driver ready state.
