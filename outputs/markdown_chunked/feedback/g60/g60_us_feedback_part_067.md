- Difficult to Use (complaint): (Active) cruise control : Not user-friendly / (Active) cruise control / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer explained the cruise control will operate for a short period and then shut off. Customer noted the vehicle had about 500 miles on it and he was using it for a longer trip. Customer observed that after stopping and turning the vehicle off and back on, the cruise would function again for a while and then cut out. Customer stated that while traveling he called a BMW dealer for guidance, and they advised him to perform a reboot. Customer reported that after the reboot the cruise control stayed on much longer before cutting off, but it still eventually stopped working. Writer asked whether this occurred on the return portion of the trip or if it is still cutting in and out now. Customer replied it did happen on the way back, but he has not been on the highway since returning to test it further. Customer mentioned he intends to bring the vehicle to the dealer, but because he lives far away he is unsure when he will be able to go. Writer sent a follow up email to the dealer.
