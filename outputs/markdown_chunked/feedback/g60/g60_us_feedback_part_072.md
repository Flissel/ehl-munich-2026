### G60-0981

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Gesture control : Not user-friendly / Gesture control / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the gesture control function operates inconsistently. Customer stated he is not sure if there is some kind of glitch, but it reacts too sensitively when he is not trying to use it, and then is not sensitive enough when he actually tries to activate it. Customer stated he talks with his hands, so it will sometimes switch songs by accident, yet if he actively try's to gesture to skip the track, it wont respond. Writer recommended performing a reboot of the iDrive system by pressing and holding the volume button in the vehicle for about 70 seconds. Writer stated this process will reset the telematics control unit. Writer advised him to try this to see if it helps, to at least rule out any sort of glitch. Customer thanked the writer and stated he will try this.
