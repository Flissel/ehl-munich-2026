### G60-1083

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Instrument cluster : Not user-friendly / Instrument cluster / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated she used to see the trip data and odometer by pressing the BC button on the turn signal, and now she cannot figure out how to bring them up. Writer advised that on the right side steering wheel controls there is a gear symbol inside two brackets. Writer instructed her to press that button and then use the scroll wheel and arrows to change the instrument cluster view until she finds the trip information, noting that one of the layouts also displays mileage from factory.
