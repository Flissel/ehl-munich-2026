### G60-1121

- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Traffic Light And Sign Warning : Not user-friendly / Traffic Light And Sign Warning / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when driving toward a stop sign, a small stop sign icon will show up in the head-up display, but on occasion it does not appear even though most of the time it does. Writer helped the customer move the warning timing to an earlier option and explained the path: Apps Menu, Driving Settings, Driver Assistance, Safety and Warnings, Traffic Light and Sign Warning, then choose Early. Customer was able to complete this change. Customer mentioned he uses Waze through Android Auto, and writer clarified that this feature has limitations with the Android Auto integration. Customer asked if he should primarily use the BMW Navigation so the symbol shows more consistently, and writer advised that this would be the best approach. Customer indicated he understood and thanked the writer.
