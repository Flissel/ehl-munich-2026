### G60-1130

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Mobile device, wireless charging : Not user-friendly / Mobile device, wireless charging / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the wireless charging pad does not charge his phone as well as plugging it into a computer or wall outlet. Customer stated that when he is playing music from the phone, it hardly charges and mostly just holds the battery level. Customer stated that if the phone is at about 57 percent when he gets in, it is usually still around 56 or 57 percent when he gets out. Customer stated that the phone will charge when it is not in use, but when it is running music or maps it basically only maintains the charge. Customer stated the phone becomes warm when using Android Auto while it is on the wireless pad. Writer explained the pad is not intended for fast charging. Writer explained the vehicle can fast charge the phone when a cable is used, and that there is a Type C cable near the cup holders that will charge much quicker. Writer explained that using Android Auto while wirelessly charging makes the phone warmer because it is doing both tasks. Writer explained that plugging the phone in is more practical, will keep it from heating too much, and will charge it faster.
