### G60-1151

- Source: Source A
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect, Difficult to Use, Likes, unknown_feedback_type
- Signal types: complaint, praise, unknown

Classifications:

- Likes (praise): Audio mode, sound : Like: sound / Audio mode, sound / Like: sound
- Defect (complaint): Audio mode, sound : Noise / Audio mode, sound / Noise
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Center console, front : insufficient storage space / Center console, front / insufficient storage space
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Wheels / tires : Noise / Wheels / tires / Noise
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> i've had the car for about 4 months and i'm splitting my thoughts into pros and cons.

pros:
- driving dynamics: really strong acceleration and power delivery, steering is accurate and easy, the car feels nicely planted in corners, and the steering weight is spot on.
- technology: screens have great clarity and quality, ui is fast, apps and info are solid, speakers/sound system are excellent, the iconic sounds are fun, and the mybmw app integration and features plus the ambient lighting are all big positives.
- styling: love the interior dash design and the look of the front fascia, especially the new lights and the kidney grille.

cons:
- brake pedal feel when slowing down isn't right.
- sound insulation around the b pillars is really weak; tyre/road and other cars' noises are way too loud for a 5 series, so the b pillars on both sides need extra deadening.
- rear legroom should be better for a car this long.
- not enough storage: missing rear seat pockets and nothing under the center console.
- rear lights don't look as iconic as the front, and those two silver bits on the lower bumper just seem out of place.
