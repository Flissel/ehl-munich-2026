### G60-1291

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation : Not user-friendly / Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that with this EV he was surprised by how much the car begins to slow when he lets off the accelerator, and that this feels quite different compared to an ICE vehicle. Writer explained that a big part of that sensation is energy recuperation, or regenerative braking. Writer advised the vehicle starts converting kinetic energy and charging the battery as soon as the accelerator is released. Customer stated the explanation at purchase could have been better and that this new feel is going to take a little getting used to.
