### G60-1329

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, climatization : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, climatization / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported the heater seems to switch on by itself even though the climate control display shows everything as off. Customer stated she can feel warm air coming out of the vents while the system indicates it is completely off. Customer noted this has happened about three times over the last month and is not a daily issue. Customer confirmed it only occurs with the heater blowing air, not with the air conditioning. Customer stated these incidents took place when it was hot outside, with the windows open, and she had not turned on the heater or A/C manually. Customer added that heat was coming from all the vents. Writer asked whether she had turned the climate controls off herself or if they were already off when this happened, and customer replied they were already off. Writer suggested it might be residual heat from the engine, and customer mentioned she will take the car in for a checkup if the problem starts happening more often.
