### G60-1349

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Not comfortable / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Not comfortable

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is unhappy with the auto engine start stop feature. Customer reported it feels rough and makes the whole car shudder when the engine comes back on. Customer acknowledged it is intended to help with fuel economy and emissions, but said he ends up using sport mode to prevent the engine from shutting off, which he feels uses more fuel. Writer recommended trying sport individual with all options set to comfort as a workaround. Customer advised he already tried that and when the engine setting is in comfort the engine still stops, so he feels the workaround is pointless. Customer commented he is so frustrated with this change that he is thinking about selling the car and switching to a different brand. Customer added that overall he really likes the car, but he strongly dislikes the way the engine turns off.
