- Likes (praise): Head-Up Display : Like: function / Head-Up Display / Like: function
- Difficult to Use (complaint): RSC tire : Not user-friendly / RSC tire / Not user-friendly
- Likes (praise): Rear view camera : Like: function / Rear view camera / Like: function
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> we swapped a 2021 530 and a 2022 530 for two 2026 530s, and the new cars feel similar in overall quality, ride, and cabin noise. we are still learning our way around the touch screens and the menu structure, and i figure we will get the hang of it over time. i am still trying to convince myself that touch screens are truly as convenient as the old knobs and buttons, since they demand more visual attention instead of simple finger feel. the mapping functions and the heads-up display are better than before, and i really like the route graphics, especially how a real-time camera view pops up when approaching an intersection where you need to turn. nobody was able to tell me why run flat tires are no longer standard equipment. there are two letdowns for me: the removal of the small compartment to the left of the steering wheel, which used to be an ideal spot for sunglasses, and the redesign of the door storage areas into an odd shape, unlike the previous design that could hold an extra drink container. i tried the complimentary feature that lets the car pull itself into a saved parking spot, but i did not feel it was accurate enough to guide the car into my garage without risking contact with the wall on one side or the other vehicle on the opposite side.
