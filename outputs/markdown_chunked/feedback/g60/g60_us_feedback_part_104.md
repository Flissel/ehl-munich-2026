Customer comment:

> Customer stated the wireless phone charger sometimes shows slow charging without an obvious reason and that it never seems to charge at full capacity, noting this might be tied to using an iPhone 13. Writer stated the wireless charger provides 15 watts and explained it is weaker than using a cable in the USB ports, mainly intended to maintain charge rather than actively charge, especially when the phone is connected to CarPlay. Writer added that depending on the phone model and case the wireless tray may not charge at all and only hold the existing charge, though if the phone is not on CarPlay and not in use some charging can occur. Writer stated cables are much faster for charging than the wireless tray.
