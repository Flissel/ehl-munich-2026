### G60-1453

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology : Malfunction / ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he experienced glitches with the content display, starting when the vehicle had under 200 miles. Customer stated the screen would sometimes freeze or the content would fail to load properly. Customer stated he brought the vehicle in for service and the system was reprogrammed. Customer stated the work was done by a BMW engineer at the 210 building at the Ohio office. Customer stated he was informed there is a problem with Service Pack 22 that was causing the behavior he saw. Customer stated he was also told that using Digital Key Plus instead of the key fob might be contributing to some of the issues. Customer stated he works for BMWNA, which is how he was able to have a BMW engineer examine his vehicle. Customer stated the reprogramming was completed the same day and he has not experienced any further issues.

Since service was performed at the BMW Headquarters, we do not have service notes.
