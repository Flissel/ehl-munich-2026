- Source: Source B

- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Drive Recorder : Not user-friendly / Drive Recorder / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he had noticed something about video recording but was not clear on what it actually did. Writer explained that the BMW drive recorder can capture a recording if there is an accident or theft, but the customer needs to configure the feature in the vehicle by opening the apps, selecting the drive recorder app, and confirming the user warning. Writer advised that in crash-related situations such as sudden braking or approaching another vehicle, the system will store the recording automatically. Writer further advised the customer can trigger a manual recording by pressing and holding the park assist button.
