Customer comment:

> 1. forward collision warning and automatic emergency braking
this car is supposed to come with standard forward collision warning and automatic emergency braking, but those features basically do not work. i have contacted your technical support several times, and although they made some tweaks, the system still does not function.

in june 2025 i brought the car in again for checks. the technical advisor, michael, rode with me and we tried different driving situations and maneuvers, yet the system never reacted at all, no warning lights, no alerts, no braking. i also tried it myself on i-25 at highway speeds, deliberately getting within about 5 feet of other vehicles, and it still failed to activate.

the sensor area has been kept clean and clear, we have gone through all the settings and looked at the owner's manual, but the problem continues.

i realize the collision avoidance is only meant to assist, not replace careful driving, but i got used to relying heavily on a similar setup in my previous lexus. i expected the bmw system to work at least as well and would like ongoing help to find a fix.

2. homelink garage door opener
the second issue is with homelink. two of your technical advisors came to my home to try and pair it with my garage opener, but they couldn't get it to sync. later i brought in a professional opener technician from liftpro, who finally managed to create a connection, yet the homelink is still unreliable and only works now and then.

most of the time when i come home, the car will not open the garage door. i end up parking, going in through the front door, and opening the garage by hand, which basically defeats having the integrated system in the first place.
