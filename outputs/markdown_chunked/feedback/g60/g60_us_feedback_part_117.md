### G60-1601

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Resting state after door opening : Not user-friendly / Resting state after door opening / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when they switch the car off, the radio does not immediately shut down and only turns off after the vehicle has been off for a while, and commented there should be a way for the radio to power down as soon as the car is turned off. Customer acknowledged this was a bit of nitpicking. Writer explained there is a setting in the doors and windows menu under the unlock and lock section, and directed Customer to open that menu while in the car. Writer noted the option is located all the way at the bottom and that, when enabled, everything will turn off when the driver door is opened. Writer suggested activating this feature would probably make Customer very satisfied. Customer expressed appreciation for having something new to play with later and remarked that this was fantastic.
