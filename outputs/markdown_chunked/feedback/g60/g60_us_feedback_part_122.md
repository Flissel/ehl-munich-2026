### G60-1665

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Welcome Light Carpet : Malfunction / Welcome Light Carpet / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he bought the welcome light animation subscription through the my garage portal and it has not worked at all since he activated it. Customer stated the dealership suggested doing an iDrive reboot, but after that it still would not work. Customer stated the dealership seemed to know very little about this feature. Writer confirmed on their end that the welcome light animation subscription shows as active, reviewed the situation further, and called the customer back later the same day to troubleshoot. Writer asked whether the vehicle was signed in with the same BMW ID used to purchase the service. Customer stated that it is, noting he sees his name and photo when he gets in. Writer explained that this is a function on demand feature and that the vehicle must be driven at least 30 minutes after booking the service. Customer stated he has driven well over 30 minutes since then. Customer stated he already drives the vehicle a lot and it currently has about 1100 miles. Writer then advised performing an update of apps and services, since that is what keeps the vehicle synchronized with anything changed in the my garage portal or connected drive. Writer asked if the customer was next to the vehicle, and the customer stated he was not. Writer still outlined how to update apps and services in the car: select MENU, go to system settings, scroll all the way down, and choose update apps and services. Writer stated this should resolve the issue. Customer indicated he understood and thanked the writer for the help.
