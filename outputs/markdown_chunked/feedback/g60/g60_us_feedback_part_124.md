### G60-1692

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Connection fault / Apple CarPlay / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has been experiencing connection problems with his iPhone 14 Pro Max on AT&T staying linked to the vehicle when using Apple CarPlay. Customer stated he plans to have his service department investigate the connection issues while the car is in for a recall. Writer advised that there are specific steps to follow that can help improve the connections. Writer advised: 1) Update the phone to the latest iOS, as phone software (iOS 16.6, 16.6.1) has been causing connection problems in the vehicle. 2) Remove the phone from the vehicle's Mobile Devices menu/app. 3) Remove the vehicle from the phone's Bluetooth menu. 4) Go into the CarPlay section in the phone's general settings and tap Forget This Car. 5) Perform a hard reset (force restart) of the iPhone by: a. pressing and quickly releasing the volume up button, b. pressing and quickly releasing the volume down button, c. pressing and holding the side button, d. releasing the side button when the Apple logo appears. 6) Restart the BMW headunit with a telecommunications reboot: a. make sure the engine or motor is turned on, b. press and hold the volume button for 70 seconds, during which the iDrive screen will turn off and on, and c. once the screen turns off the third time, release the volume button. 7) Confirm that phone Wi-Fi is turned on but not connected to another source. 8) Add the device again in the iDrive Mobile Devices menu/app and follow the prompts between the vehicle and phone. 9) Connect to Apple CarPlay when asked. Writer also emailed these instructions to the customer.
