# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-1752

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Engine Start (My BMW App / MINI App) : Not user-friendly / Remote Engine Start (My BMW App / MINI App) / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that Remote Engine Start through the My BMW app consistently fails to start the engine and usually shows a message that the vehicle cannot be remotely started at this time. Customer added that Remote Engine Start via the key fob typically fails too and that he has not checked for My BMW app updates recently.
Writer recommended updating the My BMW app to the most current version. Writer also instructed the customer to make sure the Remote Engine Start disclaimer is accepted in the Climate Menu under General settings, Pre-conditioning, Remote Engine Start, Start engine for climate control, and then confirm the disclaimer. Writer advised that if Remote Engine Start still does not work after that, the dealer should be asked to further diagnose the issue.

### G60-1753

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation : Not user-friendly / Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer explained that the regenerative braking on the i5 feels quite different from the iX. Customer stated that on the iX, regen kicks in immediately when you lift off the accelerator, at any speed or in any situation. Customer stated the i5 instead uses adaptive regenerative braking, changing its behavior depending on the vehicle speed, surroundings, and location. Customer mentioned that at times the i5 will coast unexpectedly. Customer reported it has been challenging to adjust to how the i5's regenerative braking operates compared to the iX. Customer said it would be preferable if there were some way to fine-tune how the adaptive regenerative braking functions. Customer added that overall they do think adaptive regenerative braking is better, but it remains difficult to get used to after being accustomed to the iX setup.
