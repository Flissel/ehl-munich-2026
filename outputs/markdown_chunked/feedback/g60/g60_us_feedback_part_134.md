### G60-1831

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Apps (in-car) : Malfunction / Apps (in-car) / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the built-in Spotify application in the vehicle is unreliable. Customer stated that when they try to play music via the in-vehicle Spotify app, the system just shows a spinning icon like it is connecting but then fails to actually play anything. Customer stated they fully deleted and reinstalled the app, but the problem continues. Customer stated this happens at least a couple of times per week. Customer stated Spotify functions correctly on their phone, on their home Sonos setup, and when using their phone for audio in the car, although that requires taking the phone out manually. Customer stated their previous 2024 BMW 540i and other BMW vehicles such as their X7 and X5 did not have this issue with the Spotify app. Customer stated this situation is frustrating because they simply want to hear their music.
