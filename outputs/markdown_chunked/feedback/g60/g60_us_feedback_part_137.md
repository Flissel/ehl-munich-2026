## Market: US

### G60-1855

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Electric Range : Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range) / Electric Range / Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range)

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he was puzzled that the range display shows different mileage estimates at 100% charge depending on where the car is charged. Customer stated that when charging on the road at an Electrify America supercharger, the screen indicates 307 miles at 100% charge. Customer stated that when charging to 100% at home, the display shows 259 or sometimes 270 miles. Customer stated he does not understand why a full charge would result in different range readings. Writer explained that the range estimate reflects how the vehicle has been driven previously. Writer explained the number continually adjusts based on factors like freeway versus city use, air conditioning or heating, driving efficiency, and other conditions. Writer explained that the system tries to predict the miles available from the current charge using past driving data. Customer acknowledged the explanation and stated this was the only issue he wanted to ask about.
