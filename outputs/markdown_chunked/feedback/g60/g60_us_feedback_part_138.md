### G60-1875

- Source: Source B

- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Interior design : Customer request: design / Interior design / Customer request: design

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the interior becomes extremely hot after the vehicle sits in the sun for roughly 10 minutes. Customer reported the cabin temperature reaches around 110 degrees and that the dashboard gets hot enough to burn when touched. Customer stated this level of heat is higher than in any other car brand they have owned. Customer mentioned they do not live in a desert climate and expressed worry about how the vehicle might perform in even hotter regions. Customer acknowledged this suggests the interior is tightly insulated and sealed, but stated the severity of the heat buildup is unacceptable for a vehicle costing about 75000 dollars. Customer further stated they believe this is not a one-off case and that all vehicles of this model likely experience the same issue.
