> i really do like this car: the way it puts the power down, the handling, the ride, how it soaks up rough patches, the stance and style, and the excellent paint. but there are a few things that need rethinking. the multimedia/infotainment setup is overly complicated, like the software team did it just because they could. complexity isn't what defines luxury; real luxury should be easy to use, and this bmw system isn't. there's also a quality issue on the dash that jumps out at me whenever the sun hits it, making me wonder about bmw's qc on that part. then there's the brutal, eye-burning glare from the gloss black on the center console. did nobody drive this in bright daylight? i'm seriously thinking about having it wrapped in matte vinyl to calm it down. since nothing else inside is gloss black, i keep asking myself, why do it there? and why are there no pockets on the backs of the front seats? those are very useful and should have been included. it feels like a bean counter cut them, and that was a bad decision.
