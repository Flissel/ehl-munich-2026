### G60-2029

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation : Not user-friendly / Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated it was hard to get used to Adaptive Recuperation. Customer stated he normally expects the car to coast when he releases the accelerator, but in this vehicle it feels like it is braking when he expects coasting. Writer stated Adaptive Recuperation is designed to recover some of the energy that would be lost while the vehicle is coasting and use that energy to charge the battery. Writer stated that if the customer does not like Adaptive Recuperation, he can reduce the strength by going to the Apps menu, then Driving Settings, Drivetrain and chassis, Adaptive Recuperation, and selecting the desired setting.
