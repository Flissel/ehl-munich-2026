### G60-2113

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Navigation : Not user-friendly / Navigation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that she finds the navigation system terrible. Customer stated that she will often enter an address just to see the estimated arrival time, since she already knows how to get there and mainly uses it as a timing tool. Customer stated that for one destination the car predicted it would take 40 minutes, but in reality it took her 26 minutes to get there, and under normal conditions it usually only takes her about 20 minutes to reach that same place. Customer stated she could accept it if the system were routing her onto side roads because of traffic, but she believed taking the highway should have gotten her there closer to her usual time. Writer advised the customer to review her navigation settings and verify that the highway option is enabled so routes can factor that in, and Customer stated that she would check those settings.
