### G60-2211

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, lock / unlock : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, lock / unlock / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated she is uncertain whether she can accidentally lock herself out of the vehicle. Customer explained that she has left her key inside the car, walked away, and then the vehicle locked itself automatically. Customer stated that she and some others tried this out in the morning and they started thinking the behavior might be due to a spare key being stored inside the house in a drawer close to the garage, with the car picking up the signal from that spare key and locking. Customer asked if she could end up locked out if she left her purse in the car that contains both her phone and her key. Writer explained that the vehicle should not lock when the key is inside and that the car is supposed to sense the key remaining in the cabin. Writer clarified that if an additional key is near the vehicle, the system may detect that second key as well, which could trigger the locking behavior. Writer suggested that the customer can use the BMW app on her phone to open the vehicle if needed and noted that there is an icon in the app specifically for unlocking. Writer confirmed that the vehicle is intended not to let the customer lock herself out because it should register that the key is inside. Customer stated she understood the explanation about the spare key being close by and causing the locking and also now knows about the BMW app unlock feature as a backup solution.
