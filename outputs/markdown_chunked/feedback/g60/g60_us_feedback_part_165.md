### G60-2245

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): BMW ConnectedDrive upgrades/ MINI CONNECTED upgrades : Not user-friendly / BMW ConnectedDrive upgrades/ MINI CONNECTED upgrades / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer mentioned she assumed her car already had Remote Start like others she knew, but when she tried to find it, there was no option. Customer asked if this vehicle is equipped with Remote Start. Writer clarified it is available as a bookable upgrade in the connecteddrive store. Writer further explained she would receive a one month free trial once she confirms it in the connecteddrive store in her app. Customer then asked if there were different levels because she really wants that feature. Writer advised there is an unlimited level and that prices would be visible on the customer side. Writer stated that once the upgrade is purchased it will download to the car and then needs to be activated through the climate control settings. Customer acknowledged this information.
