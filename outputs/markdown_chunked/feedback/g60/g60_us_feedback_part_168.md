Customer comment:

> Customer asked what influences the vehicle's range. Writer further advised that preconditioning the vehicle while it is plugged in can significantly help increase the range. Writer clarified that preconditioning warms the battery using power from the wall instead of drawing from the battery itself, which in turn helps the overall range.
