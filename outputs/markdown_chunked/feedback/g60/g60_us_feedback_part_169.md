### G60-2305

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60

- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Parking Assistant : Not user-friendly / Parking Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he was confused about how to use the self parking function. Writer advised to push the parking button on the center console, which turns on the parking cameras and lets the vehicle begin looking for a spot while driving. Writer advised the customer needs to be traveling at around 20 miles per hour for it to search for a space. Writer advised once the system detects a suitable space it will notify the driver and an icon representing the space will show up on the display. Writer advised the customer can then stop the car when the alert appears, tap the icon on the screen, and allow the vehicle to take control and park itself. Writer advised the driver may still operate the brakes to help avoid other obstacles and slow down the maneuver if needed.
