- Source: Source B

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Drivetrain : Drive power/driving performance not OK / Drivetrain / Drive power/driving performance not OK

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has owned 2 BMW 540i models before. Customer stated the performance of his 530i is not as strong and that the car does not feel as peppy. Writer stated the 540i has a slant 6 engine, while the 530 uses a Twin Turbo 4 cylinder engine. Customer stated that when he moved from his 2018 540 to his 2021 540 he noticed a performance increase, and he expected the 530 to deliver a similar improvement. Customer stated he does not feel the 530i is as responsive. Customer stated he drives in both Comfort and Sport Mode, so he feels he is comparing "apples to apples".
