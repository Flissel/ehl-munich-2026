- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Lane departure warning : Not user-friendly / Lane departure warning / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated she does not want to shut off the function that keeps the car in its lane. Customer stated when she tries to move into another lane the car pulls her back into the current lane. Writer explained the system is meant to let the vehicle change lanes if the turn signal is turned on, so it should not pull back in that case. Using RVG, writer advised that lane departure warning and steering intervention can be disabled, and instructed her to go to: Apps>Vehicle>Driving settings>Driver Assistance>Safety and Warnings>Lane Departure Warning, then switch it off.

### G60-2354

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Software Upgrade/RSU : Not user-friendly / Remote Software Upgrade/RSU / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported he has received a couple notifications about a Remote Software Upgrade in the My BMW App. Writer checked and advised that he appears to be on the last step, and that the vehicle only needs roughly a 20 minute period parked to finish the installation. Customer mentioned he is currently miles away on a trip while the car is sitting in his garage. Customer asked if the upgrade could be initiated from the app. Writer confirmed and walked him through where to locate it. Customer then stated he was able to schedule the upgrade for 1am. Writer advised it might not complete successfully while the car is in the garage because it has to connect to satellites. Writer further advised that if it does not work, he can simply move the vehicle outside and restart the upgrade.

### G60-2356

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Ambient light : Customer request: function / Ambient light / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that prior 5 series models let them adjust interior lighting independently of the driving mode, but the 2027 model no longer offers that. Customer explained the earlier car allowed choosing the interior light color without linking it to comfort, eco, or sport, whereas now the lighting automatically changes with whichever mode is selected. Customer expressed this is annoying. Writer advised that colors can be customized in comfort mode, while eco mode shows a bluish tone and sport mode shows red.
