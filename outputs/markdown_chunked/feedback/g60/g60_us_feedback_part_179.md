### G60-2454

- Source: Source B

- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, lock / unlock : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, lock / unlock / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they prefer that all doors unlock upon approaching the vehicle instead of just the driver door unlocking. Customer stated this is a minor concern but it is a bit irritating when trying to enter the car. Writer explained how to reach the locking settings by pressing the home button, then selecting All Apps, then Vehicle, then Doors and Windows, and then the Lock Unlock menu. Writer advised that the first option appears as a dropdown labeled Driver Door Only and instructed the customer to change it to All Doors. Writer confirmed that once this is updated, all doors will unlock from then on. RVG Attempted.
