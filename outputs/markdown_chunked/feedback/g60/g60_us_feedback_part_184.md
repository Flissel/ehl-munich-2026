### G60-2524

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Not user-friendly / Apple CarPlay / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated his iPhone is currently connected to the vehicle through Bluetooth. Customer stated that when he chooses the Apple CarPlay option, a message appears warning that phone features like calls and audio will only be available via CarPlay. Customer asked what this message meant. Writer explained that changing the connection from Bluetooth to Apple CarPlay slightly alters how the system works. Writer stated that Apple CarPlay copies the phone interface but functions separately from the BMW iDrive. Writer stated the warning simply indicates that once you switch to Apple CarPlay, phone calls must be made through the CarPlay interface, not the BMW iDrive, until the connection is changed back to BMW Bluetooth. Customer acknowledged.
