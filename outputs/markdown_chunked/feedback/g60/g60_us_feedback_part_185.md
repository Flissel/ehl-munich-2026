- Difficult to Use (complaint): (Active) cruise control : Not user-friendly / (Active) cruise control / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> I had a minor problem with Cruise control/adaptive cruise control - DTU/controls poorly located: "I eventually realized it was not really a malfunction, just a change from how I canceled cruise on my 2021 compared to this 2024. My 2021 had a RES/CAN button on the steering wheel, but the 2024 does not. Now I see that it depends on when I press the ON/Off for cruise: while actually driving it will shut cruise off, but if I am coasting after braking or coming down a highway off ramp, the same ON/Off acts like Resume. That Resume behavior made the car accelerate in the off-ramp curve when I was not expecting it. Now I know how and when to use the cruise control icon on the left side of the steering wheel and understand the difference from the 2021 to the 2024."
