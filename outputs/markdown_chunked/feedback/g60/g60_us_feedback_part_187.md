Customer comment:

> this is my fourth bmw, and this 530ix really does not let me down. the tech makes it easier to drive and adds safety with things like lane warning, blind spot alerts and the little beepers. the ride itself is very smooth.
my last sedan was a 2013 535ix, so a lot of the differences stand out. for one, the doors on this car feel much lighter than before. i liked the heavier doors, and these new ones sound kind of hollow. the audio system also powers up as i walk up to the car, whether it is sirius or pandora from my phone. i am old school, i always turn the radio off before shutting the car, so this is annoying. plus, when other people get in, they might end up hearing something that is not appropriate for them.
then there is the auto start/stop, which in my opinion cuts in way too quickly. after just an instant of being stopped the engine shuts off, and with summer heat that drop in a/c feels uncomfortable. turning it off every single time i start the car is really inconvenient.
lastly, i have to admit i did not pay much attention to the trim level when i ordered this one, and i miss a few of the features that came with the nicer package on my previous car. since this one is leased i will probably just hand it back at the end and go for an upgraded model. i love my cars, the dealership, and the service department/manager, and i do not see myself switching to somewhere else.
