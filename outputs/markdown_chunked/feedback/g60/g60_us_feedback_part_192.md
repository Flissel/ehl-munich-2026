### G60-2642

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Malfunction / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when he is driving in Efficient Mode, he always switches off Auto Start/Stop but it still seems like the car is shutting off. Writer asked if he notices this with any of the other Driving Modes. Customer stated no, he sets it to Efficient Mode every time he drives. Writer asked how he is turning Auto Start/Stop off. Customer stated that when he gets in, he presses the MY Modes button, selects Efficient Mode on the screen, then swipes down from the right side of the display and taps the Auto Start/Stop icon in the upper right corner to disable it. Customer was very clear that he repeats this process every time he starts a trip. Writer asked if he could go out to the car, but customer stated he could not. Writer advised that on the Instrument Cluster there is a power gauge on the right side of the display, and when Auto Start/Stop is still active and he is stopped, it will show 0 or Ready. Customer confirmed this. Customer mentioned the dealership Genius had offered to come to his home for a tutorial. Writer advised that they would normally schedule an RVG session, but in this particular case it would likely work better for the Genius to sit in the car with him so he can feel it and see it together.
