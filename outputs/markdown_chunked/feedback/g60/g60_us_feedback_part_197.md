### G60-2712

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Braking : Noise / Braking / Noise

Customer comment:

> Customer stated a grinding noise happens during slow stops, mainly from the front, probably on the driver side. Customer mentioned it began within the first 200 miles, and the car is now at around 1,100 miles. Customer rated the severity at 5.5/10, compared with 9/10 on the previous 2025 model he traded in. Customer stated it is more noticeable on level or downhill stops and does not occur on uphill stops. Customer said it only happens in normal drive mode (D), not in Sport mode, and only at low speeds when coming to a stop. Customer reported the issue as intermittent, occurring about 5 out of 10 times. Writer scheduled an RVG call to capture the noise on video, which the customer then rescheduled for 2 weeks later. Writer called back and customer stated that after the temperature dropped significantly, both the brake noise and the RMP issues disappeared.
