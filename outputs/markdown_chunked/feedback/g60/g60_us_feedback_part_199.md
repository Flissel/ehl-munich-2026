# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-2737

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Parking Assistant : Not user-friendly / Parking Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that even with the recent improvement, the self-parking function is still below par. Customer stated that, given the quality of the cameras and sensors on the car, the feature should be ten times better than it currently is and that it does not meet BMW standards. Customer stated the system had been worse before the latest update, and is now improved because it can also locate a parking space on the opposite side, which it could not do before, and this makes the customer feel more confident in its ability to find a spot. Customer stated that using self-park to go into a space forward is very difficult on this car. Customer stated it is hard for the system to select the actual space that is intended. Customer stated the process should be made more user-friendly, and that this has led to almost never using the function anymore to park the vehicle. Customer stated a preference for parking manually and mentioned other drivers have honked because it takes too long when trying to park with the system. Customer stated the feature is inaccurate and does not send the car where it is supposed to go. Customer stated that in normal parking situations time is often limited. Customer stated this has been a major fault. Customer stated that, as a Tesla owner as well, the BMW setup is immeasurably worse than Tesla for the same feature. Customer stated the Tesla system lets the driver press on the desired parking spot on the dashboard and the car handles it, and that it is much quicker and performs much better. Customer stated yet the optical hardware on the Tesla is inferior to BMW's, and therefore would have expected superior results from the BMW system and from German engineering.
