# G60 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G60-2840

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Braking : Not user-friendly / Braking / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that the car seems to use electric braking rather than hydraulic braking, and said it is basically impossible to come to a smooth stop without the car jerking forward. Customer described that even when pressing the brake pedal very gently and gradually, the vehicle still comes to a sudden halt, almost like slamming on the brakes, with no way to get a calm, gentle stop. Customer indicated the odometer is around one to two thousand miles. Customer added that when driving in sport mode the problem shows up less often. Writer explained that during the early break-in phase, usually up to around two thousand miles, the brakes can feel especially strong and powerful, which may make the braking feel more aggressive. Writer recommended doing some firmer braking in safe locations so the brakes get more accustomed to normal stopping. Writer also suggested turning off the Auto Hold feature to check if that changes how the braking feels.
