- Wants (unmet_need): Drivetrain : Customer request: function / Drivetrain / Customer request: function
- Difficult to Use (complaint): High voltage battery : Not user-friendly / High voltage battery / Not user-friendly

- Defect (complaint): Vehicle, lock / unlock : Malfunction / Vehicle, lock / unlock / Malfunction
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> overall it is an outstanding car. there are just a couple of things that really feel like cost cutting, and i honestly do not get why those choices were made. the biggest one is that the car cannot acclimatize while on the ice and instead will only use the main hybrid battery, which makes absolutely no sense. it comes across like somebody just did not bother to push the right software update. that behavior also hurts the practicality for people who commute. you are forced to drive in a way that saves enough of the main battery so you can still use the acclimatization settings before your next trip.

the other irritation is that you cannot unlock the car using the rear passenger door handles. at close to $80,000, it feels like they could have added two more touch sensors to make locking and unlocking simpler. overall the experience is great, but there are definitely a few things that make you wonder if they were driven by engineering or by a bean counter.
