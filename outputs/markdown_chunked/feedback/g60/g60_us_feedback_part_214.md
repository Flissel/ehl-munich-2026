### G60-2955

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Public charging / external providers : Not user-friendly / Public charging / external providers / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when he was charging on a 350 amp DC Electrify America charger, the vehicle charged at 120kW for roughly 12 minutes. Customer stated that after those 12 minutes, when the battery reached 60% state of charge, the charging speed dropped down to 85kW. Customer stated he had been expecting to see around 250kW instead of only 85kW. Customer stated he did not try any other charging stations in that area. Writer stated that factors such as heat or high demand from other vehicles charging could have influenced the charging rate. Writer stated it might be helpful for the customer to call Electrify America during the charging session so they can check why he is not getting the expected charging speed. Writer stated that Electrify America's phone number is normally printed right on the charging station.
