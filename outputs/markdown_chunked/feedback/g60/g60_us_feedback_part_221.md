### G60-3048

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Shift paddle : Not user-friendly / Shift paddle / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer mentioned she saw a boost paddle on the left side and was unsure what it actually did. Writer explained that if she pulls that paddle, "sport boost" should pop up in the instrument cluster along with a countdown timer. Writer further advised that if she presses the accelerator before the countdown reaches zero, the vehicle will give a stronger, quicker acceleration than normal. Writer stated that the function can be fully canceled by holding down the right paddle. Customer then wanted clarification on how the regular shift paddles work. Writer informed her that she can press and hold the left paddle while the car is in drive or sport mode to enter manual shifting. Writer advised that the display will show either D plus the current gear or M plus the gear number. Writer clarified that in D mode, if the driver is not pushing the car hard and the paddles are not used for a short while, the transmission will go back to automatic shifting on its own. Writer noted this does not apply in M mode, where the driver must pull and hold both the left and right paddles so the car can return to automatic operation.
