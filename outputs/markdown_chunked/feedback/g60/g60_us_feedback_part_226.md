### G60-3109

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation : Not user-friendly / Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that the adaptive braking behavior is confusing to him in terms of how consistently it works. Customer described that when he is driving and coming up to a street light or a stop sign, or when he turns on the blinker, the vehicle will begin to brake for him even before he actually presses the brake pedal. Customer stated that when he uses the blinker the way the car brakes seems logical in relation to how far away he is from the turn he is about to make, and the system then applies braking. Customer explained it took him some time to get used to this, because he feels like he should be doing the braking himself and now, when he is braking and the car is braking at the same time, it can feel a bit jolty. Customer commented that he has to plan for the adaptive braking that is going to occur.
Customer noted that sometimes at a stop sign or street light the car clearly detects the stop sign or light, because it will show a small symbol of a stop sign or street light in the heads-up display, but he said that does not happen very frequently. Customer estimated that at a stop sign maybe around 20% of the time the system will recognize it and display it in the heads-up display, but most of the time it does not, which he finds confusing. Customer stated it is similar for street lights, and that he has only seen it show a red light symbol in the heads-up display once or twice when he comes up to a red light, while the vast majority of the time that symbol does not appear.
Customer stated that when the car takes over braking in some situations but not in other situations, it is a bit unsettling for him, and he would prefer to understand what the underlying logic is and to know more consistently what the car is going to do and what it is not going to do. Customer added that the amount of adaptive braking the car applies without him touching the brakes does not feel consistent as he approaches stop signs or street lights. Customer said the system seems more predictable when he turns on the blinker, and in those cases he can better anticipate how much braking the car will do adaptively, and he has adjusted his own braking to account for that.
Writer asked whether he was in any kind of driver assistance mode when he noticed the traffic light symbol. Customer replied that he was not, that he never changed the mode, and that he has only used the assisted driving mode on the freeway one time, which is not what he is referring to in this situation. Writer shared from her personal experience that she has seen the little icons appear for a red light or stop sign when she was approaching a red light and was not slowing down quickly enough, and that is when she saw them pop up. Writer explained that if she is already slowing down adequately, then the symbols do not show up to give a quick alert.
