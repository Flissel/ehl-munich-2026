Customer comment:

> my first experience with a bmw 7 series was as a passenger back in the early 2000s, and it really left a lasting impression on me. that memory stayed with me and eventually led to buying my first bmw 7 series around 2008. a few years later, i traded that car in for something more suited to family needs, but the feeling of driving a bmw never left. i promised myself i would own another bmw someday.
that time is now, and i'm extremely happy with my 2025 bmw 540i xdrive. the excitement i felt in my early 20s is exactly what i feel again in my early 40s, and even more so. the overall bmw experience is special: the comfort, the ride, the styling, and the distinct personality of the 540i xdrive make it unforgettable.
there are, however, a couple of areas that could be improved. first, the front-facing camera. i get why it's positioned where it is, but aesthetically the placement isn't appealing. from the side, it really sticks out and makes you wonder why it couldn't have been put somewhere more subtle. the bump disrupts the smooth, clean lines of the front bumper. second, the front driver and passenger usb-c charging ports are awkwardly placed. you really have to shift your body noticeably to plug in properly, and a more convenient location would be much better.
all that said, i'm thrilled every time i get behind the wheel of my 540i xdrive and plan to enjoy it for many years.
