### G60-3246

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Personal eSIM : Not user-friendly / Personal eSIM / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he tried to configure the Personal eSIM through iDrive, but it seemed like it was trying to create a new Verizon account. Customer stated he already has an unlimited Verizon plan. Writer explained that activating the Personal eSIM will add data services through Verizon and show up as a separate device on his Verizon account. Writer advised instead to set up the eSIM from the My BMW app by going to "Profile", then 3rd Party Services, choosing "Personal eSIM", tapping "Activate eSIM", entering his mobile number, and then selecting "Check with provider" so he is directed to Verizon to complete the activation.
