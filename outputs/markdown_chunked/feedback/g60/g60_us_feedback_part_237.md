### G60-3272

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Mobile device, wireless charging : Malfunction / Mobile device, wireless charging / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that a few weeks ago she began hearing a rattle from the phone charging tray whenever her phone is placed there. Customer confirmed she is using an iPhone 16 Pro in a hard plastic case. Customer confirmed she previously had a soft silicone case and thinks the noise may have started around the time she switched cases, but she has also tried the phone without any case and still hears the rattle. Customer stated the charging tray area and cupholders are completely empty when the sound occurs, and it happens whether the vehicle is idling or being driven. Customer stated the rattle is like a wind-up type noise, similar to a lot of coins shaking, even though the area is clear. Customer confirmed the phone itself does not move or vibrate in the tray and functions normally, and she has not yet tested with a different phone. Writer advised having the service center check this, especially if the issue also appears with other phones.
