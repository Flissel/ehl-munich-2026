### G60-3321

- Source: Source B

- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Electric Range : Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range) / Electric Range / Information / Accuracy insufficient (electric range)

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the estimated driving range shown has gone down recently from around 440 to 450 miles to about 380 miles. Customer asked if charging the electric battery would increase the range and acknowledged they have not been consistent with charging since the electric range is only about 30 miles. Writer explained the range shown is an estimate that changes based on driving conditions and habits, including whether driving is mainly in-town or on the highway. Writer clarified that in-town driving is less efficient and lowers the estimate, while highway driving is more efficient and can raise it. Writer advised that not charging the battery will affect the overall range displayed.
