### G60-3399

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Not user-friendly / Apple CarPlay / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer explained he uses hearing aids that pair to his phone via Bluetooth. Customer stated that sometimes when he is wearing them, they will end up connecting to the vehicle and he hears all of the vehicle sounds and audio through the hearing aids. Writer used RVG to look over the Bluetooth devices associated with his vehicle. Writer noted that only the customer's iPhone was stored or connected in the Bluetooth list. Writer informed the customer that, since his phone is connected to the vehicle with Apple CarPlay, it could be Apple CarPlay that is causing the audio to be routed into the hearing aids. Writer advised him to review the settings on his iPhone. Customer stated that the next time this occurs he will try to troubleshoot and see how it is happening.
Customer mentioned that he was not wearing the hearing aids at the time of the call.
