- Source: Source A

- Signal types: complaint, praise, unknown, unmet_need

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology : Malfunction / ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology / Malfunction
- Difficult to Use (complaint): ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology : Not user-friendly / ConnectedDrive and Infotainment, overall technology / Not user-friendly
- Difficult to Use (complaint): General enquiry: customer feedback : Information / accuracy insufficient / General enquiry: customer feedback / Information / accuracy insufficient
- Defect (complaint): My Modes / driving experience : Malfunction / My Modes / driving experience / Malfunction
- Wants (unmet_need): Vehicle, access and exit : Customer request: function / Vehicle, access and exit / Customer request: function
- Likes (praise): Vehicle, lock / unlock : Like: function / Vehicle, lock / unlock / Like: function
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> the "driving machine" part really is "ultimate", but the infotainment setup badly needs some refinements. bmw seems to have no real way for owners to send in feedback or ideas to fix the counterintuitive parts of the system, and the geniuses at the dealer told me there isn't any channel for that.
one specific problem is the auto lock/unlock when you come up to the car or walk away. that function is great anywhere except at home. in my garage i'm walking past the car over and over while i work, and each time it goes on and off repeatedly. that cannot be good for the car, and the only way i could stop it was to switch the entire feature off.
this exact issue shows up as a common complaint online, and i found out that i-drive 8.5 has a setting to disable the comfort access "at home" only. that would be a perfect fix, but when i tried to change my settings so i could actually enjoy the feature i paid for, i discovered it only exists on 8.5 in the 7-series, not on the same 8.5 in my 5-series.
for me that is a real "deal-breaker". bmw is aware, or absolutely should be, that this is a problem. they clearly already have a solution, but 5-series owners are blocked from using it. i have a whole list of other issues and would gladly go over them with someone who truly cares. they're simple, common-sense fixes i'd provide for free... if there were any line of communication.
