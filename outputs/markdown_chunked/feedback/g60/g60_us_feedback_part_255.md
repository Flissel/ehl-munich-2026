### G60-3518

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Software Upgrade/RSU : Not user-friendly / Remote Software Upgrade/RSU / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer called asking what steps to follow when a Remote Software update appears. Customer mentioned seeing the notification both in the My BMW App and in the car, and was unsure how to handle it or confirm it went through correctly. Writer explained that once the message shows up, it first has to be downloaded, and asked which option the customer had used. Customer replied he did the update through the My BMW App. Writer clarified that the update in the app must download from the app to the phone, and after that it will upload to the vehicle using wifi. Customer then asked if the vehicle itself needed to be connected to something for this, and writer advised that it does not. Writer stated the phone must be connected to a wifi network, not using mobile data, in order to upload the update to the car. Customer indicated he understood. Writer further explained there are certain preconditions that must be satisfied or the Remote Software Upgrade will not complete properly. Writer went over that the customer needs to make sure the download is finished, the vehicle is not on an incline, is in park, and the engine is switched off. Customer confirmed all of those were true and that the Remote Software Upgrade had already been completed successfully, and said he had only been curious about the process.
