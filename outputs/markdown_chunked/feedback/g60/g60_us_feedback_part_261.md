### G60-3605

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Interior acoustics : Noise / Interior acoustics / Noise

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that rattles are much more noticeable in BEVs and that this vehicle has several distinct rattles, most noticeable in the 40-60kHz range. Customer stated the rattling has been present from day one and occurs primarily when playing music at certain tones, but also at times when driving over bumps. Customer stated the areas affected include the back deck behind the passenger seats, the panel between the rear dome light and rear brake light near the roof, the sunroof control panel, and the air vents with small flaps by the subwoofer in the trunk. Writer advised having the service center inspect these specific spots to verify that all parts and panels are installed as intended. Customer acknowledged.
