- Difficult to Use (complaint): Electric Range : Range, not OK / Electric Range / Range, not OK
- Likes (praise): Handling / Riding : Like: function / Handling / Riding / Like: function
- Defect (complaint): High voltage battery, preconditioning : Malfunction / High voltage battery, preconditioning / Malfunction
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Parking Assistant : Not user-friendly / Parking Assistant / Not user-friendly
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> the car is really fantastic overall. it has the best ride of any vehicle i've owned, and i really like the acceleration. the level 2 driving *mostly* works very well, though i haven't really played with the self-parking yet.

my issue with the self driving is that it reacts too slowly when another car moves into or out of the lane ahead. once a slower car pulled into my lane and then had to brake because of traffic. the bmw 550e kept going at a higher speed as that car merged, and when they hit their brakes my car had to slam on the brakes hard. i honestly thought i was going to crash, but luckily it didn't happen.

the hybrid setup is great because it cuts my fuel costs a lot. the downside is that if the destination is a fair distance away, the car uses *all* the available battery to get there, so you can't then precondition the car for the drive back.

while i did have the money to buy it, i still feel it's a bit on the pricey side, and that makes me less willing to recommend a somewhat overpriced car to friends.
