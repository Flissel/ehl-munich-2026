### G60-3689

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) : Connection fault / Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is struggling to keep his phone connected to the vehicle through the BMW app when he is away from the car, not while he is actually inside it. Customer stated that when he is remote, the app has trouble connecting and will not display current info for the car, including the power level and the lock and unlock functions. Customer stated he saw a message earlier saying he needed to enter the vehicle to connect, and that the app reported there was an update available. Customer confirmed he is using a Samsung S23 with Android 16 on the T-mobile network. Writer advised removing the BMW app from the phone and reinstalling it, and explained that once he logs in it should only request to pair a single time, which should fix the problem. Writer further advised doing the longer system reboot by holding the volume scroller on the center console for about 70 seconds, until the screens go blank and then cycle back on around three times, to force a reboot of the vehicle's telecommunications and its communication with the phone. Writer advised that one of these two steps should get the phone and car working together again, and noted that Bluetooth can remain on while performing the reboot.
