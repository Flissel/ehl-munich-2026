- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) : Not user-friendly / Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they needed help sending a destination from the My BMW app into the car's navigation and mentioned they are not very tech oriented. Writer walked Customer through opening the My BMW app and choosing the new 550E from the two vehicles shown, a 2021 and the new 2025. Writer instructed Customer to tap the second icon along the bottom, described as a circle with another circle inside, to get to the map. Writer asked Customer to pick a destination and demonstrated with Middleton Medical, which appeared about nine minutes away and roughly three miles. Writer then guided Customer to use the send to my BMW button and verified that Customer received the destination sent confirmation. Writer told Customer to go out to the vehicle to retrieve the destination and noted a pop-up should show when starting the car. Customer was able to access the sent destination successfully.
