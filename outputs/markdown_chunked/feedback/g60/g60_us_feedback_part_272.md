### G60-3738

- Defect (complaint): Manage / pair mobile device (Bluetooth) : Connection fault / Manage / pair mobile device (Bluetooth) / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the Bluetooth connection keeps dropping off and on. Customer stated he has to re-establish the connection each time to get his phone to hook up again via Bluetooth. Writer asked whether the customer was currently near the vehicle. Customer stated he was not with the vehicle at that moment. Writer was not able to launch RVG. Writer advised the customer to have the phone forget the car and have the car forget the phone, then set up pairing again as if it were a brand new device. Writer further advised the customer to perform a 70 second reboot of the iDrive by holding down the volume knob. Customer stated he would try both of those steps to see if they improve the Bluetooth issue. Writer asked what type of phone he is connecting. Customer stated it is an Android S23. Writer asked which carrier he uses. Customer stated Verizon. Writer asked if any other devices are paired over Bluetooth in the car. Customer stated there is also an iPhone paired, but it is rarely used.
