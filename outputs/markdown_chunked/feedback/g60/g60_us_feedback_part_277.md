### G60-3812

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G60
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): non-contact trunk lid opening : Not user-friendly / non-contact trunk lid opening / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the tailgate foot-activated opening does not consistently function. Writer asked if she had checked the settings to confirm the feature was enabled. Customer replied no, and since she was already inside the vehicle, writer guided her to the menu. Customer indicated both open and close foot movements were checked. Writer then asked which key she uses, and customer answered she uses her Digital Key. Writer instructed her to stand centered at the rear of the vehicle, take a step back, then kick her foot straight forward and straight back like kicking a soccer ball, and then wait about 3 seconds. Customer reported it still did not work. RVG attempted while the customer tried again, and she stated it worked once she took a slightly smaller step back. Writer suggested testing it several more times to verify both opening and closing worked. Customer did so and confirmed both functions now operate properly.
