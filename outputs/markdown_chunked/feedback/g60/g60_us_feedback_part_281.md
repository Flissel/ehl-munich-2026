- Source: Source B

- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

- Difficult to Use (complaint): non-contact trunk lid opening : Not user-friendly / non-contact trunk lid opening / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has only been able to get the trunk to open once using the kick-to-open function. Customer stated he is using the digital key plus on his phone and holds the phone near the BMW badge on the rear of the car. Customer asked if the kick feature should operate when using his phone as the key. Writer advised that it is intended to work. Writer asked if unlock on approach was enabled. Customer stated that when he walks up to the car it unlocks automatically. Writer asked where he was kicking under the bumper. Customer stated he kicks under the middle of the bumper. Writer suggested performing two separate kicks in a row while standing about an arm's length away from the back of the vehicle. Customer stated he has a service visit at his dealership today and will have them demonstrate this feature. Writer asked if he was aware he could also close the trunk using the kick feature. Customer stated he knew that, but since he cannot reliably get the open function to work, he has not attempted the kick-to-close.
