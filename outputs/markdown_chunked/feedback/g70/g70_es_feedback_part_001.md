Customer comment:

> i am happy with the new car. it is clearly more comfortable and in general more interesting than the older series i had. still, there are some drawbacks i really cannot ignore: - once the speed goes past about 110-120 km/h, the car is not well insulated anymore, wind noise becomes obvious and disturbing even though it has the special package. - at night, the driver's main rearview mirror gives a blurry picture, lights from cars behind and strong light sources look smeared, vertical lines show up and this makes driving harder. - the air conditioning will not run when the ignition is switched off. - there is no way to turn off the lights while the car is on (for example if you just want the ac working in a parking lot). - every single time you start driving you have to switch on the battery conservation function again. - the driver has no glove compartment now. before it was located to the left of the steering wheel. there is also no smaller extra glovebox like on the f01. - the driver's joystick/control stick feels very rigid when choosing left/right/up/down. - the driver's seat fails to go back to where it was before the car was shut down. - the cup holders do not offer cooling or heating at all.
