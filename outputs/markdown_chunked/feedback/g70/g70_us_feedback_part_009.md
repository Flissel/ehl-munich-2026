# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-0125

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Malfunction / Apple CarPlay / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that CarPlay frequently fails to work. Writer confirmed the customer is using an iPhone 14 PRO Max on AT&T with software version 16.4. Writer advised rebooting the phone by fully powering it off and turning it back on, and also suggested un-pairing the phone, removing it from iDrive, then pairing again. Writer recommended restarting the iDrive by holding the radio power button for 30 seconds. Customer stated he has already performed those troubleshooting steps. Writer then suggested a one-time hard reboot of iDrive by holding the button for 70 seconds and recommended a visit to the dealership. Customer stated that when CarPlay acts up, he can still listen to music. Customer stated CarPlay sometimes does not appear, that the phone shows up but CarPlay is missing, and at other times he cannot select the CarPlay checkbox. Customer stated that when he cannot check the CarPlay box, he is also unable to delete the phone from iDrive. Customer stated CarPlay does work properly on some occasions.
