Customer comment:

> from a mechanical standpoint the car is excellent, in line with what i've come to expect from my previous seven bmws. it drives smooth, is very quiet and feels luxurious.
all of the problems i've run into are tied to the software.

route planning -
there is room to improve the in-car navigation system.
1-you are unable to plan a charging route starting from anywhere other than where the vehicle is currently parked, and you cannot set a starting charge level different from the present state of charge. that makes it impossible to pre-plan the second leg of a longer trip. this information should be something you can input.
2-the route suggestions are nowhere near optimized. for instance, i recently drove from fairview to bayport. the nav gave me a plan with charging stops showing an eleven and a half hour trip. having made that drive before (though not in an ev), i knew there was a more direct way. by simply dragging the route magnet through a different city, the recalculated route ended up about an hour shorter. why should i have to fix that manually?
3-the mybmw app ought to provide ev-specific route planning, similar to what my wife gets with her mercedes app for her ev.
4-route planning on bmwusa.com is basically useless. try it. choose range and route, i7 e50 20", put in a starting point like 210 harbor view rd, seaview beach fl and a destination like lakeside toyota of fairview. the result is just a line on the map with state names. no city labels. no identified charging stops. nothing that actually helps an ev driver. on a previous attempt it also showed a "?" button that, when pressed, only told me i would need to charge somewhere on the way. that is not helpful.
5-i ended up having to build a spreadsheet and use my own numbers to plan the journey. i've asked the local "genius" for guidance, but the way bmw's supporting software is designed is beyond what she can assist with.
6-i'm still hoping the mistakes are mine and that you can show me a better way to use these tools. but as i see things right now, the limitations of bmw's route planing tools aren't just unhelpful - they feel insulting. maybe that would be borderline acceptable in an economy car, but not in the i7.

seat positioning -
most of the time, after i unlock, get in, start the car and buckle up, the driver's seat moves correctly from the comfort access spot to the stored position 1 for driving. however, roughly 20% of the time it doesn't move at all. and once it even slid further back instead. usually tapping the "1" button will fix it, but sometimes that button has to be held down, and sometimes only the seat controls on the door will get it to move. i suspect there might be some link to how and when i clear the nuisance disclaimer message.

updates -
not long ago i was away for about a week. the first time i drove the car after coming back, i found a lot of things had been reset. my seat memories had vanished. the custom wake word i'd set was no longer active. saying "hey bmw" did nothing, even though those options still showed as selected in the menus. there were also new displays in the instrument cluster when driving with assist plus.
it would help if:
1 - existing user settings were kept intact when an update is installed.
2-a simple message popped up at startup letting the driver know the vehicle might behave differently than it did on the last drive.

thank you for looking at my comments on this car. it's unfortunate that these frustrations exist. out of all the bmws i've owned, this is the one i am most eager to drive.

i would really like to get a response to the issues i've mentioned.

mark ellis
