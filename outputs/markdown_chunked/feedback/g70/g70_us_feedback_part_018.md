Customer comment:

> this car will not suit everyone. the ride is smooth and quiet, the seats and audio are excellent, and the fuel economy and performance are impressive for such a large vehicle. the accent lighting is something I truly enjoy. the downside is the slightly isolated feel of the ride and some tech that comes across as intrusive. the accelerator pedal has a bit of a sticky feel, and the wave-your-foot trunk opener hardly ever works. the nav system is dreadful. it does not seem set up for americans, and its guidance is often inconsistent and wrong: saying "bear left" when there is no reason, then not saying it when there should be; sending me onto blocked or unfinished roads even though the map later shows them as blocked; telling me to stay on the same road ahead when nothing is actually changing; using bear left/right when you are really turning onto a different road and it should say "turn left onto...". it also does not announce when it is rerouting. there is better technology out there.
