### G70-0266

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Audio mode, sound : Not user-friendly / Audio mode, sound / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated her device is connected to the vehicle with Bluetooth. Customer stated that on some calls she can hear the other person, but they cannot hear her. Customer stated she believes it may be switching over to the rear audio zone automatically, though she is not certain because she does not really understand that function. Writer explained the audio zone can be adjusted manually, and that it should normally switch on its own depending on how the call is answered. Writer stated that answering from the control display should set the audio zone to the front, and accepting the call through the touch command should move the zone to the rear. Writer advised removing the phone in the mobile device menu and pairing it again. Writer stated that re-establishing the Bluetooth connection can sometimes help in situations like this. Writer tried to go over the steps to change the audio zone manually if needed, but the customer already knew how to do that.
