### G70-0398

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Drivetrain : Malfunction / Drivetrain / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated his car has been sitting in the dealer's service department since around 1/8, and it has basically been out of his hands this whole time. Customer stated the dealer, while running remote diagnostic checks, picked up an issue with the air and fuel mixture. Customer stated the store still does not have a clear answer on what is actually causing that air/fuel mixture problem. Customer stated he only noticed the check engine light turning on, and besides that he did not feel anything odd in the way the vehicle was driving. Customer stated the car had about 560 miles on it when he dropped it at the dealership, and now, while it is in their possession, the odometer shows roughly 902 miles. Customer stated he has no idea when he is going to get the vehicle back from the dealer.

Update from roughly 2/5/25:
Customer stated his overall experience with this vehicle so far has been poor. Customer stated the car has been tied up in service for around 24 days. Customer stated that when the vehicle only had about 100 miles, the Check Engine Light came on for the first time. Customer stated his service technician believed the concern was related to the O2 sensors. Customer stated that when the dealership contacted BMW engineering for support, engineering did not want to approve replacing those sensors at that stage and instead told service to clear the fault codes and have the customer drive the car more to see if the Check Engine Light would return. Customer stated the light did in fact come back on, and he then had to bring the vehicle back into the service department. Customer stated he ended up driving about 800 extra miles with the Check Engine Light illuminated. Customer stated that eventually service replaced the O2 sensors to resolve the issue. Customer stated they were also told the vehicle might require a software update if the Check Engine Light comes back again. Customer stated the way BMW handled the troubleshooting has been very frustrating and that BMW needs to listen more closely to what the technician on the ground is recommending. Customer stated that the second trip into service could have been avoided if BMW had listened to the technician who was diagnosing the car the first time. Writer asked how the vehicle has behaved since he picked it up after the repair. Customer stated he has had the car back for about a week and it is running OK, but he is still worried the check engine light might reappear.
