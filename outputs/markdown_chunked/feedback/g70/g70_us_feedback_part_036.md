### G70-0489

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Comfort Access : Not user-friendly / Comfort Access / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that because she has only one Key Fob, when she hands that Key Fob to a valet, they will leave it inside the vehicle, and then the doors end up locking the Key Fob in the car when she walks away carrying her Digital Key. Writer stated that to stop the Digital Key from locking the doors in this situation, she can turn off "Passive Entry" in Apple Wallet by tapping the BMW Digital Key, then tapping the lowercase "i" in the top-right of the phone screen, and toggling off "Passive Entry". Writer stated this keeps the Digital Key on her phone, and "Passive Entry" can be easily switched back on after she picks up the car from the valet. Writer stated another option is to activate the Service Card by placing it on the smartphone tray and following the activation prompt on the Control Display, then giving the valet the Service Card to use as the key for the vehicle.
