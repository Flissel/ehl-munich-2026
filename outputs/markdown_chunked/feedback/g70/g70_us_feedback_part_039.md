### G70-0531

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Charge high-voltage battery : Too slow / Charge high-voltage battery / Too slow

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has a regular one way drive of about 190 miles, and has to stop to charge one or two times, losing roughly 20-30 minutes per session. Customer stated he can always earn more money but he cannot gain back time, and he does this trip every month. Customer stated the car tops out at 190 watts of power and asked if it could go higher. Writer advised he should be able to open the apps menu, then charging, then ac limit, and there adjust the current limit. Customer stated that setting was already at its maximum. Writer checked and noted this vehicle is limited to 195 kw, and relayed that to the customer. Customer acknowledged this and thanked the writer.
