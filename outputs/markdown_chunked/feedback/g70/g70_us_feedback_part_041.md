### G70-0553

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Remote Engine Start (Vehicle Key ) : Connection fault / Remote Engine Start (Vehicle Key ) / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that during the second week of owning the vehicle he repeatedly received a warning saying the remote start could not be used. Customer stated that on the first visit to the dealer they were unable to locate the cause. Customer stated that on the second visit the dealer reached out to BMW engineers in Germany for help. Customer stated that shortly after he bought the car, BMW released a software update that resolved the problem. Customer stated the dealership installed this update and he had no further issues after getting the vehicle back from service. Customer stated he did not know the mileage when the problem first appeared.

Customer later called back to report the same issue had come back again. Customer stated he intends to return the vehicle to service.

Service Notes from second service:
