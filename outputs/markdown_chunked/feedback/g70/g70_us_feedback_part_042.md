### G70-0567

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Controller operation : Malfunction / Controller operation / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer states the iDrive controller and the buttons around it occasionally stick and either fail to respond or trigger the wrong feature. Customer states that, for instance, when navigation is running and a call comes in, pressing the Nav button to resume guidance will sometimes end the call instead. Customer states the surrounding buttons are constructed as a single piece, so they sometimes get stuck, will not register a press, or will select an adjacent function rather than the intended one. Customer states the push-down function of the iDrive controller itself also sticks and does not operate properly. Writer asked if the customer might be accidentally hitting the wrong selection. Customer states they are confident they are pressing the correct control. Customer states this problem has existed since they picked up the vehicle and appears to get worse as they use it more. Customer states they plan to have the dealer evaluate it at their next service visit. Customer states the odometer is between 1K-2K miles.
