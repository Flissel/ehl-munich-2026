### G70-0579

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Mobile device, wireless charging : Not user-friendly / Mobile device, wireless charging / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the Wireless Charger will not work with certain phones, including his iPhone 17 Air. Customer explained the phone has a big camera that sticks out, so the charger does not detect it. Writer asked if he uses a phone case. Customer replied no, and said he is sure the camera is the problem because when he pushes the phone down, it starts charging. Writer asked if he had tested another device to confirm the charger is operating correctly. Customer stated he has not, but mentioned it is not a major concern. Writer acknowledged this and asked if he had ever seen the LED indicator; customer answered no. Writer described where the LED is found and what each color indicates. Writer advised that if the light turns red, he should contact a dealership to arrange a service visit. Customer stated he was unaware of this and will watch for the LED going forward.
