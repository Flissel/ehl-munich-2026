### G70-0667

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Not user-friendly / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he was worried about the vehicle being a Mild Hybrid. Customer stated he had an electric vehicle before that caused a lot of motion sickness. Customer asked how often the electric motor would be on and what speeds it comes on. Writer advised the electric motor assists the combustion engine, during the time the combustion engine shuts off when slowing toward a traffic light or stop sign. Writer advised it may typically come on under about 7 MPH. Writer advised it is usually not noticeable when the electric motor is operating. Writer advised the vehicle will begin to accelerate with the electric motor and then the combustion engine takes over. Customer asked if, when the vehicle is stopped in traffic for a while, the engine will turn back on to recharge the battery. Writer advised that it will, and also that the engine comes on when the climate control needs to heat or cool the cabin again.
