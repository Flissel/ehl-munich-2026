### G70-0765

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): High voltage battery, preconditioning : Not user-friendly / High voltage battery, preconditioning / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer asked what preconditioning means. Writer explained that preconditioning works kind of like remote start, but it not only heats or cools the cabin, it also warms or cools the battery. Writer advised that using preconditioning while the vehicle is still plugged in will be important in the winter and colder months to help preserve battery life and keep a decent amount of range. Customer asked at what temperature he should start making sure to precondition. Writer advised that around 40 to 50 degrees would be a reasonable point, basically whenever climate control is needed to drastically change the inside temperature of the vehicle. Writer noted that the owner's manual does not give a specific sugesttion.
