# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-0771

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Driver profile : Malfunction / Driver profile / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is trying to set up his personal profile so that when he gets in the car, the AC temperature and all other settings return to where they were left. Customer mentioned the profile appears on the screen with a welcome message and his name, but the settings are not restored to his previous values. Customer explained that his preferences are not being saved, including the AC and the seat position, and that he has to press seat memory button one again for the seat to go back where it should be. Writer asked if there is another driver involved and whether the customer is using a smartphone or Apple Watch as the key. Customer confirmed he uses a smartphone and is the primary driver about 99 percent of the time. Customer stated his wife does not have a profile set up on the 7 series. Customer stated he plans to visit the dealership when he has time to get assistance with the profile setup. Writer advised the customer to set all settings again, including the seat, make sure they are stored in the profile, and then lock the car upon exiting. Writer verified in Dealer cockpit that the permissions are configured correctly. Writer then asked if the customer also has an Apple Watch or carries the digital key card. Customer confirmed he only has an Apple phone and does not use an Apple Watch. Writer explained that once everything is configured and the vehicle is locked, the system should recognize the profile each time. Writer recommended going to the dealership if the issue continues. Writer also suggested performing an iDrive reboot by moving the car out of the garage, leaving the engine running, and pressing and holding the radio volume button for 70 seconds. Writer explained the screen may turn black as many as three times during that period and instructed the customer to keep holding the button for the full 70 seconds, then allow it to boot up to see if the settings are saved. Writer stated this procedure forces the system to update to the latest version since sometimes it is searching for settings that no longer exist. Customer confirmed he understood all the steps and stated he would try the reboot. Customer then stated he could not stay on the call. Customer stated he would contact the dealership later today or tomorrow to arrange an appointment for next week to address the profile settings concern.
