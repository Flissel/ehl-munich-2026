# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-0840

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Personal eSIM : Malfunction / Personal eSIM / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that from the time he took delivery, roughly 70% of the times he starts the vehicle the eSIM icon in the iDrive menu bar shows that the Personal eSIM is not configured and not working. Customer stated that when this occurs he cannot stream anything to the Theater Screen in the rear. Customer stated that in these instances, the iDrive Tel > Mobile devices list has the Personal eSIM entry highlighted with the note "Setting up personal eSIM" beneath it, and it never progresses or finishes. Customer stated he has gone to the dealership Genius multiple times and that the Genius adjusts something in the myBMW app to get it working again. Customer stated the fix does not last and the eSIM soon goes back to not functioning. Customer stated that he has been able to connect the eSIM and stream to the Theater Screen on some occasions, but that most of the time it fails because the eSIM never fully completes setup and connection.

Writer checked AIR and confirmed vehicle integration level G070-23-03-553 is newer than the -540 level specified for AT&T Personal eSIM. Writer connected via RVG and verified the issue: the sim card symbol with an exclamation mark in the menu bar indicated an eSIM fault. Writer confirmed the customer's description that the Tel > Mobile devices screen shows Personal eSIM highlighted with "Setting up personal eSIM" that does not change. Writer confirmed the customer was signed into his BMW ID / driver profile. Customer verified in the myBMW app that the Personal eSIM was activated on AT&T and stated he has an unlimited plan for the eSIM. Writer verified the customer's iPhone 14 is on iOS 16.5.1 and that the myBMW app is current and operating correctly.

Writer guided the customer to iDrive Menu > Vehicle apps > System settings > Update apps and services. Customer selected Update apps and services, and iDrive reported the vehicle software is current. Writer then walked the customer through a 30-second iDrive reset. Writer had the customer use the myBMW app to deactivate the eSIM from the i7. Writer next directed the customer in iDrive to Menu > Apps > Personal Hotspot > Connect new device. When the QR code appeared on the Control Display, customer stated the dealership Genius had previously skipped this step and done something different in the myBMW app, though the customer could not remember the details. Writer instructed the customer to scan the QR code. Customer stated the QR code did not proceed to move his phone further through the setup flow.

Writer then advised the customer to re-activate the eSIM using the Personal eSIM section in the myBMW app. Customer entered his phone number but the process stopped, and the app indicated he needed to add service. Writer guided the customer to select the eSIM device in iDrive and choose Activate internet access. Customer followed this, and his phone was forwarded to the AT&T login page. Customer signed into his AT&T account and tried to re-activate the eSIM on the i7, then ended the RVG session. Customer reported that on the AT&T site he was being asked to add service and that he could not re-activate the eSIM this way.

Writer explained that the problem appeared to be related to the customer's AT&T account, since AT&T was requiring added service for eSIM activation. Writer recommended that the customer contact AT&T to confirm his account and eSIM plan are truly unlimited and compatible with the Personal eSIM. Customer insisted the fault was with BMW. Writer still advised the customer to perform a 70-second iDrive hard reset to restart the telematics unit, even though the symptoms pointed more toward an AT&T account issue than telematics. Writer also recommended the customer return to the dealership so the Genius could repeat the same method they had used before to start the eSIM without involving the QR code or AT&T portal. Writer encouraged the customer to ask the dealership service department to check for any possible hardware defects due to the repeated nature of the problem. Customer did not specify what he intended to do next. Writer advised that the customer could reach out again with further questions or updates, but that the immediate next steps lay with AT&T or the dealership. Customer acknowledged.
