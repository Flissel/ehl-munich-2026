### G70-0882

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Not user-friendly / Apple CarPlay / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they suspect it is related to using Apple CarPlay, as when Apple CarPlay is connected the voice control button does not seem to function. Writer explained that Apple CarPlay uses its own operating system in the vehicle, which takes over voice control for communication and navigation. Writer advised that other voice commands, such as climate control, can still be used. Writer further advised that when connected to Apple CarPlay, for communication or navigation the voice control button needs to be pressed and held for about 3 seconds until the Siri tone is heard, then the request can be made through Siri. Customer thanked the writer for the information.
