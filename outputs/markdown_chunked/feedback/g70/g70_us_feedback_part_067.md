### G70-0887

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, climatization : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, climatization / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that both she and her husband experience the air conditioning shutting off intermittently. Customer stated she will press the air conditioning button and then a short time later it turns itself off again. Writer explained that pressing the air conditioning button will only switch the A/C on or off, and that the temperature still needs to be set if the Automatic Program is enabled. Writer stated the Automatic Program might be automatically switching off the climate control when the cabin reaches the chosen temperature. Writer stated that if this is not what she wants, the Automatic Program can be turned off so she can manually adjust the blower and other functions. Writer added that if the climate control seems abnormal, the dealer would need to further diagnose it. Customer stated she will look at the climate control settings and may visit the dealer on a different day.
