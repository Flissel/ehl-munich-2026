Customer comment:

> it is an excellent car in terms of ride comfort, steering feel, braking and the basics, but there are several "features" that make using it somewhat unpleasant.

1) with the car in drive, the doors will still open if someone accidentally touches the door open button. this feels dangerous. that function really ought to be disabled whenever the vehicle is in gear. and honestly, why have power opening doors at all when simple manual door handles have worked perfectly for roughly the last century.

2) the interior manual door release handles are positioned in places that are very hard to reach.

3) the auto stop-start turns the engine off before the car is fully stopped. in stop-and-go traffic this is irritating and inconvenient because you lose engine braking as soon as the engine shuts down.

4) there is no way to defeat the auto stop-start in the normal drive mode. i know using sport mode avoids this, but i do not want to drive around in sport mode.

5) the engine automatically switches off when you put the transmission in park and open the driver door. this is a hassle when briefly stopping to let a passenger out and then continuing the trip.

6) the horn will blast loudly while the car is in park if the driver steps out with the key to open the trunk or similar. i really dislike being yelled at by the car.

7) the front passenger seat slides backward toward the rear passenger as they open their door to get out, which makes it harder for them to exit. comfort exit is turned off in the settings, yet this still happens.

8) the driver seat moves backward and then instantly forward again when the transmission is put in park and the seatbelt unlatched, even though comfort exit is disabled in the seat menu.

9) the sharp, perforated metal grilles over the door speakers have led to several passengers and drivers snagging and breaking fingernails while feeling around for the door panel buttons.

10) there is no way to close the trunk lid from inside the cabin.

i am assuming other drivers have raised the same complaints and that bmw can deal with at least some of them soon with a software update. of course, the locations of the door release handles and speaker grilles would need a physical redesign.
 michael doran
lead chauffeur
fairmont holdings
