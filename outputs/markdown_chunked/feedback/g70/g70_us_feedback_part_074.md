### G70-0972

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Permanent switch-off not possible / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Permanent switch-off not possible

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is unhappy that in the 7 Series he can only switch off MSA by putting the vehicle into Sport Mode. Writer advised that MSA can instead be turned off in the iDrive system. Writer directed him to click on "MENU">"Vehicle apps">"Driving settings">"Drivetrain and chassis">"Auto Start/Stop" and then choose the preferred setting. Writer further explained that on the Auto Start/Stop page, he should "hold your finger on the screen for a second." Writer stated that when the shortcut option appears, he should select yes. Writer added that the customer can then pull down from top to bottom on the iDrive display and select that shortcut. Writer confirmed that MSA cannot be programmed to be default off.
