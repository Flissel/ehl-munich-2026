- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Software Upgrade/RSU : Not user-friendly / Remote Software Upgrade/RSU / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he received a message in the My BMW app that a Remote Software Update was available for the vehicle, but after checking through the menus he could not figure out how to start the update. Writer advised he should go to Menu > Vehicle > System Settings, then scroll down to find "Remote Software Update" and follow the prompts to install, and informed him the vehicle will be unusable for about 20-30 minutes while the update runs.

Customer stated that one aspect he prefers on his Tesla is being able to initiate software updates directly from his phone without searching through menu options.
