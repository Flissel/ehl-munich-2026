### G70-1023

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Traffic Camera Information : Not user-friendly / Traffic Camera Information / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has been signed up for Traffic Camera Information since buying the vehicle, but has never seen any Traffic Camera alert appear. Customer stated he even deliberately drove through an area with known Traffic Cameras and still did not get any alert in the car. Customer stated he had used Traffic Camera information on his previous BMW and that application functioned correctly. Writer had earlier advised the customer to update Apps & Services due to an Apple CarPlay concern, noting this could also address the current issue. Writer observed the vehicle VIN shows an active Traffic Camera information subscription through 4/20. Customer was unable to complete the Apps & Services update while on the phone, so the writer connected the customer with BMW Assist to continue troubleshooting and to rule out any backend problems with this ConnectedDrive service.

### G70-1026

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): non-contact trunk lid opening : Customer request: function / non-contact trunk lid opening / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer stated Tesla uses a camera sensor with proximity for the kick-to-open trunk feature and that BMW should look into using something similar. Customer stated this is helpful because it detects how far you are from the tailgate so it will not open when you are too close, and it also makes opening much easier since you can stand anywhere behind the trunk instead of exactly in the center like with BMW. Writer thanked the customer for the idea and stated they are actively working on improving this feature. Writer asked if the customer knew you can also kick to close the trunk, and Customer stated yes, he uses that a lot when his hands are full.
