### G70-1060

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Braking : Noise / Braking / Noise

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that from the very first day of ownership, the brakes have sounded noisy. Customer stated the brakes sound wet and crunchy, almost like the pads are slipping on the brake rotors. Customer stated he particularly hears this brake noise when slowing to a complete stop, and also when his foot is on the brake pedal while turning the steering wheel. Customer stated he does not hear any squeaking or squealing from the brakes. Customer stated that when the vehicle is at a full stop and he lets off the brake pedal, it takes around 3 seconds before the car starts to creep forward. Customer stated that right before the vehicle begins creeping, it sounds as if one of the brake pads lets go from the rotor like it was stuck. Customer stated he previously took the vehicle to Metro BMW, but they did not inspect the brakes and simply dismissed the issue as normal. Customer stated he does not accept this noise as normal and has scheduled a service visit with Premier BMW on 8/15. Writer scheduled a follow-up call for 8/16.
