Customer comment:

> Customer inquired about using the automatic parking function. Writer advised Customer to start by pressing the parking camera button, and explained that in the upper right portion of the display a searching area appears where the system scans for parking lines along the passenger side while driving slowly through a parking lot. Writer further explained that after the system identifies a space and the vehicle has already passed it, the car verifies the spot is large enough, and the display in that searching area will prompt the driver to choose a maneuver option such as reverse parking or pulling straight in. Customer acknowledged they now understand how to activate and use the parking assist feature.
