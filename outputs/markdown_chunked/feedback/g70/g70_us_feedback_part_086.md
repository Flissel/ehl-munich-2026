### G70-1134

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) : Not user-friendly / Remote Services & Remote Keyless Entry (My BMW App/MINI App) / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated via email that on at least one occasion the myBMW app indicated a door was open even though all doors were in fact closed. Customer stated that repeatedly opening and closing the doors did not resolve the warning, but that the notification eventually cleared by itself. Writer advised that if this happens again, the customer should first try removing the car from My Garage in the app and then adding it back. Writer further advised that if that does not fix the issue, the customer should delete and reinstall the myBMW app. Writer advised the customer to contact us again for additional troubleshooting if the notification returns.
