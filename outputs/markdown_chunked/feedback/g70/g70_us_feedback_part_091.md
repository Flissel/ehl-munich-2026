### G70-1204

- Source: Source A
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use, Wants, unknown_feedback_type
- Signal types: complaint, unknown, unmet_need

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Drivetrain : Response characteristics: too sluggish / Drivetrain / Response characteristics: too sluggish
- Wants (unmet_need): Interior design : Customer request: design / Interior design / Customer request: design
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Navigation : Not user-friendly / Navigation / Not user-friendly
- Wants (unmet_need): Seats : Customer request: function / Seats / Customer request: function
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Seats : Not comfortable / Seats / Not comfortable
- Difficult to Use (complaint): Speed Limit Assistant : Not user-friendly / Speed Limit Assistant / Not user-friendly
- Wants (unmet_need): Wheels / tires : Customer request: design / Wheels / tires / Customer request: design
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> the car itself is quite nice, but there are a few areas that need improvement:

1-interior:
a-warm air isn't really warm enough and takes too long to build up. i have to put the temperature on max just to feel actual warmth.
b-the seats have plenty of adjustment options, but overall they are not very comfortable. maybe that's partly personal, but there should still be a default seat comfort you can expect at this level.
c-a rear-seat display should be standard at this price, not an extra-cost upgrade, especially nowadays when an oled screen is around 200$ for a consumer. if the motorization around it makes it pricey, that part should be the added cost.
d-the glossy trim shows fingerprints far too easily, and since everything is touch sensitive it's difficult to wipe it clean without accidentally changing settings.

2-driving experience:
a-for a car of this size, the steering feels a bit too wide. rear wheel steering seems like something that should be default for a premium vehicle like this.
b-overall the car is too needy, constantly popping up notifications, which can be distracting. the red light warning is not well tuned, as it will alert me even on the highway when it spots the signal for the entrance lane on the right. that is very distracting and frankly a bug.
c-switching out of autopilot isn't smooth enough, and the car behaves a bit stubborn. sometimes it decides to change lane at a very bad moment. for instance, if the navigation says i need to take an exit in under a mile, it shouldn't try to move into the opposite lane just to go faster.
d-the autopilot braking is quite harsh. even when it clearly detects the car ahead slowing, it reduces speed too late, making the brakes feel very strong.
e-when reactivating autopilot on streets with a speed limit, it should honor the current street limit, not only the setting. if i mistakenly turn the tempomat back on in a 25 zone, the car shouldn't start heading for 65.
