### G70-1223

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Vehicle battery : Warning / notification active / Vehicle battery / Warning / notification active

Customer comment:

> Customer reported the vehicle showed a low voltage battery malfunction notification that came up two times and then disappeared on its own. Customer explained the alert stated the starter battery was severely discharged and that certain power-heavy features, including remote services, were shut off to protect the battery. Customer added that the same warning appeared as a push notification on her phone at 3:55 PM on the 22nd of last month. Customer noted the car had fewer than 1,000 miles when this occurred. Customer shared that the easy entry seat position stopped functioning while the warning was present, then began working again once the message cleared. Customer stated she had no issues starting the vehicle after receiving the phone alert, and that the vehicle is currently operating normally. Customer indicated no service appointment has been set up because the warning has not returned, but if it does show up again she will arrange a visit with the dealer. Writer offered at no cost to send an over-the-air diagnostic email ahead of any possible dealer appointment and advised it would be sent to the email connected to the vehicle. Writer confirmed Bayside BMW Harborview as the chosen service facility. Writer informed the customer that the remote diagnostic is for the dealer and for our records.
