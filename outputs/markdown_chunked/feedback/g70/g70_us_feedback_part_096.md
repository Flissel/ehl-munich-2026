### G70-1270

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Audio mode, sound : Malfunction / Audio mode, sound / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the audio in the car keeps glitching. Customer stated it happens when he is using Bluetooth to play downloaded songs from his phone, or when he uses a USB jump drive with music. Customer stated that after driving for a few minutes the sound starts acting up, the music cuts out, and there is a beeping noise like some kind of interference, as if something is trying to break in. Customer stated that FM radio and Sirius XM both work fine without any issues.
Customer stated this only occurs with the USB jump drive or downloaded music on his phone. Customer stated he left the vehicle at the dealership a few days ago, but the dealership was not able to duplicate the problem at first. Customer stated that on the way home from the dealership the glitch started again. Customer stated he brought the car back, and this time the technician confirmed the problem. Customer stated the dealership tried deleting his phone from the system and pairing it again, but that did not resolve it. Writer confirmed the phone is an iPhone 14 PRO, with carrier AT&T and iOS 16.3 installed.
Customer stated he does not use CarPlay.
Follow up: Customer stated the dealership still has his vehicle. Customer stated that even after confirming the malfunction, the dealership has not been able to diagnose it. Customer stated the dealership recorded a video of the issue and forwarded it to BMWNA. Customer stated he then received a text from the dealership saying BMWNA instructed them to reset the master system, and that they would now monitor the car. Customer stated he has been without his vehicle for about 2 weeks and is hoping to get an update from the dealership in the next day or so.
DCS: Defect Description: Bluetooth Audio (Combo) occasional failure
