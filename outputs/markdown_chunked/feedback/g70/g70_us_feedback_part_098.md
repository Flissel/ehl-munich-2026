> my i7 has been an absolutely fantastic car so far, and the way it drives has felt perfect to me. it is extremely comfortable, and the overall quietness of the drivetrain and cabin is beyond what i have experienced with similar cars i owned before. the tech and amenities in the i7 meet or even surpass what i expect in this class. everyone who has ridden with me has been impressed with these same things. the range does drop in the winter, but for my daily use i find the range acceptable.

my only real issue with this particular car is that it arrived with bubbles in the sunroof glass, and the dealer is currently working on it. the car has been diagnosed with a manufacturer defect in the sunroof, with several small bubbles trapped between the glass layers of the panel. i am worried these imperfections could weaken the sunroof and affect safety over time, especially with temperature changes or impacts from rough or uneven roads. the team at bmw of Fairview has been great throughout all of this, showing professionalism, being transparent, and keeping me thoroughly informed while they resolve it. the problem, however, means the car needs a major teardown and repair, including removing the interior headliner, the pillar trim, the sun shade cassette, the windshield, and the driver assistance camera assembly, etc. since i have only had the car a few months, it is extremely frustrating to deal with such a big problem so early in ownership. i have barely had time to really enjoy what was supposed to be a flagship, brand-new vehicle before finding out it has to be significantly dismantled to fix this. as a long-time bmw fan and repeat owner, i have always respected the brand for its quality, precision, and innovation.

to date, the i7 has genuinely been a joy to own. in every other way it has met and even exceeded what i expect from a flagship executive sedan. the level of craftsmanship, the outstanding comfort, and the excellent noise isolation make each trip calm and very refined, which is exactly what i associate with bmw and the idea of the "ultimate driving machine." that makes this problem even more disheartening, since i expected years of worry-free enjoyment and trust in the car. with such a quiet cabin and electric drivetrain, i now fear that an invasive repair like this might never bring the car fully back to its original, factory-perfect state, and could introduce new shakes or rattles later.
