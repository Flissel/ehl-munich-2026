### G70-1315

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Electric Range : Customer request: function / Electric Range / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that due to the hybrid setup and battery, the current range and charging are limited, but he is hoping to reach a specific mileage target. Writer asked what range he has in mind. Customer stated that having at least 100 miles available would be extremely helpful. Customer stated he knows this car is rated for around 35 miles, but in real driving it is more like 25 miles. Customer stated his typical round-trip commute is about 55-65 miles, so to him a 100 mile range would completely change the game. Customer stated that with that kind of range, people would be eager to buy the vehicle since it would feel like a safer choice compared to going fully electric or depending only on gasoline.
