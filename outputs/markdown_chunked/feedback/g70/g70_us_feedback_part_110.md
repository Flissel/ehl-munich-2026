- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle safety, overall technology : Not user-friendly / Vehicle safety, overall technology / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> i have a 750e 2026, and this is my second 7 series model. from the very first day, the car has been making a repeating knocking sound from the right rear passenger side, very much like the automatic door lock noise (da-da). the dealership has been supportive, understanding, and professional while trying to track it down, and they have traced it to a valve that is supposed to push gas vapors toward the engine when a certain buildup level is reached.
this sound is distracting and extremely annoying to everyone in the car, and it happens while driving, when stopped, with a full tank, with a half tank, and so on. to me, that points to poor quality control on the manufacturer side, which is very disappointing. i highly doubt this is just a single isolated case and assume you have already heard about it. the 750e is marketed as your flagship, but with this issue it does not really feel like one, and i think it needs to be handled properly at the manufacturer level. i am also worried it could be a safety-related problem.
as a separate irritation, i would expect bmw to include safety features that even cheaper cars already have. specifically, there should be an optional audible alert for blind spot lane changes, in addition to the triangle symbol on the side mirrors. that triangle is hard to see, and a tone to warn that there is a vehicle in the lane would be very useful and much appreciated. make it an option that can be turned on or off, but at least offer it.
thank you for letting me share my disappointment; bmw management really should look into these matters. this is my third bmw, and i am starting to lose that pride in owning one. none of this is a criticism of my local dealership, they are always professional and supportive. honestly, without their effort to keep clients satisfied, i would most likely already be back in a mercedes. the dealer team truly acts like quintessential professionals and treats customers like family.
again, thanks for your time. you can reach out to me directly if you wish; coastal bmw - clearwater, fl has my contact information.
- david lane
