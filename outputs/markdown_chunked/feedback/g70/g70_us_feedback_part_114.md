### G70-1524

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Parking Assistant : Not user-friendly / Parking Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> During an RVG appointment, customer asked how to turn on Automatic Parking for perpendicular spaces. Writer explained that when getting ready to look for a spot, customer should press the parking assistance key on the center console, the one marked with a P and three lines. Customer confirmed the button was located. Writer explained this switches iDrive into the Parking view, where the vehicle begins scanning for suitable spaces. Writer stated the iDrive display will show which spots are detected in the searching area of the screen, and customer will then pick the desired space and parking style. Writer advised that iDrive will then instruct the customer to release the brake pedal so the vehicle can start the self-parking maneuver. Writer added that once the automatic parking is finished, iDrive will show that the procedure has ended and will shift the vehicle into park. Customer acknowledged the explanation.
