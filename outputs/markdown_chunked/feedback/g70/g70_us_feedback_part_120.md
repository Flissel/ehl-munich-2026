### G70-1603

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): My BMW App / MINI App : Customer request: function / My BMW App / MINI App / Customer request: function

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the My BMW APP and the car navigation both display Electrify America charging locations and show available chargers. Customer stated that after driving to these stations, about 2/3 of the chargers are either not working or already in use. Customer stated that many times when he plugs in, the charger will not start charging, even after several attempts to connect and reconnect. Customer stated he has driven away from a charger and then seen another driver in a non-BMW connect and charge successfully. Customer stated he has not contacted Electrify America to reboot any chargers. Customer stated he is worried about taking longer trips because he is unsure if he will be able to find a functioning charging station. Customer stated he would like the information in both the car and the My BMW APP to be more accurate. Writer confirmed the customer is using the latest My BMW APP version (3.7.1 25681).

Customer comment:

Customer stated the light comes on to show it has detected a spot, but he is unsure what to do at that point. Writer explained that once the light appears, shift into reverse, then turn on the turn signal and press and hold the Park Assist button. Writer noted that directions will appear on the iDrive display and advised to keep the button pressed until the maneuver is done.
