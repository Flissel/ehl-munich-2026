Customer comment:

> Customer stated she had dash problems in her previous X6 and chose the 7 Series thinking a more premium model would be free of such issues, but the same type of problem is happening. Customer stated the dash display went completely black around three times before she brought the car to the dealer about a month ago, where they performed a system enhancement/update (current i-level 25-07-551, i-level ex works 25-03-536, update pending for i-level 25-03-545) and advised this would resolve the concern. Customer stated the screen still went black again about four days ago, so the vehicle is scheduled to go back to the dealer on Friday 10/24. Customer stated a $100,000 car should not be experiencing these kinds of problems and asked to be moved into another comparable vehicle that does not have them.
