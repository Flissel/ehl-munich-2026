### G70-1692

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Panel heating : Not user-friendly / Panel heating / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the driver side door becomes warm near the armrest area when the seat heater is turned on and that the door panel cools down again when the seat heater is switched off. Customer explained they like the seat heater function but have never owned a car where the seat heater also heats the side of the driver door and believes this is a defect. Customer stated the dealer advised this condition is within specifications and considered normal. Writer asked if the customer might be describing the armrest heating technology, a feature on higher end BMWs where the door armrest and the center console armrest heat up independently when it is cold. Writer explained this armrest heating should operate separately from the seat heater and can be shut off in the iDrive system if the customer does not want it. Writer noted the vehicle uses temperature sensors and automatically turns on the armrest heating when it is very cold outside. Customer was not aware of the separate armrest and center console armrest heating feature and appreciated being informed about this functionality.
