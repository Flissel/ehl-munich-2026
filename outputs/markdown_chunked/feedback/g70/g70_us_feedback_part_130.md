### G70-1739

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation : Not user-friendly / Brake Energy Regeneration / recuperation / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer explained that right after purchase he did not like how the vehicle slowed down when he lifted off the accelerator pedal. Customer said he consulted the dealer, and the dealer reset the iDrive configuration. Customer stated the vehicle now coasts when coming off the accelerator, similar to a conventional combustion vehicle. Writer noted this feature is Brake Energy Regeneration, using the energy from coasting to recharge the battery and creating a sensation of "one pedal driving". Writer added that various intensity levels can be selected in iDrive under Apps, Vehicle, Driving Settings, Drivetrain and chassis, Adaptive recuperation, then choose the preferred setting. Customer stated he will remember this.
