### G70-1819

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Gesture control : Not user-friendly / Gesture control / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated Gesture Control will work properly at times and other times it does not respond at all. Customer stated that when Gesture Control is not working, the center "skip track" buttons also stop working completely. Customer stated he is always on the same media input when this happens and that his Driver Profile has already been set up. Writer stated to verify Gesture Control is turned on in the Apps Menu under System Settings and Gesture Control. Writer stated to ensure the gestures are made in the correct area, under the camera in the headliner and in front of the Control Display. Writer stated if Gesture Control still only works sometimes, the dealer should be asked to diagnose the issue further.
