### G70-1884

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, climatization : Effect, power not OK / Vehicle, climatization / Effect, power not OK

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is not pleased with how the Climate Control system behaves. Customer stated he originally relied on Auto mode, but reported that it keeps the fan running on a low setting the entire time. Customer stated that in all his prior cars he would simply put the temperature at 70 and the system would manage everything, whereas now he feels he has to go into Manual mode and crank the temperature all the way down to get the cabin to cool. Writer asked whether the fan might have been set to Level 1, but customer was unsure. RVG accepted. Customer then switched the car back to Auto mode and confirmed it was at Level 2 with the temperature set to 70 degrees while it was about 95 degrees outside. Customer stated that with the interior as hot as it is, he expects the climate to ramp up to full blast, but instead it stays gentle and he ends up sweating. Customer stated that for him to actually notice cooling, he must change to Manual mode and select Max AC. Customer stated he never had to rely on Max AC in his last vehicle, a Mercedes 560. Customer said he did not have additional time to talk and mentioned he would call again later to resume the discussion.
