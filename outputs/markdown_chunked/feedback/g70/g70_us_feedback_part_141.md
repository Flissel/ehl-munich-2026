### G70-1895

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Drivetrain : Response characteristics: too fast / Drivetrain / Response characteristics: too fast

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they previously had a 2020 740i and now drive a 2023 760i. Customer stated the change in acceleration is something they have to get used to. Customer stated they are unable to accelerate gradually. Customer stated that with only a slight press on the accelerator pedal the car takes off too quickly and they experience whiplash. Customer stated their neck is now sore. Writer advised that the 60i has significantly more power compared to the 40i. Writer recommended the customer adjust the head rest so that during acceleration their head can rest comfortably instead of being forced backward. Customer stated they have never used the head rest and always sit very straight. Writer advised adjusting both the back rest and head rest to help reduce the sensation of the body being pushed backwards due to the vehicle performance. Customer expressed appreciation for the guidance and stated they will definitely experiment with the seat and head rest settings.
