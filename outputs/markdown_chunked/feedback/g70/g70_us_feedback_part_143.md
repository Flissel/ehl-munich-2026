### G70-1925

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Automatic doors : Not user-friendly / Automatic doors / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that the automatic door opening feature feels too sensitive and also too slow. Customer explained that after pressing the button they have to step back, otherwise the door will not open, and even when they do move back there is still a long pause before the door actually opens. Customer stated this behavior makes them want to just pull the door open by hand, which defeats the purpose of having it open automatically. Customer expressed a desire for improvements to both the sensitivity and the speed of the automatic door operation. Writer advised that on the new i7 the automatic door opening has been updated and now operates about two seconds faster.
