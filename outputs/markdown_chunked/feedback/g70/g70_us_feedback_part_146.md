### G70-1956

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Interior mirror, garage door opener : Not user-friendly / Interior mirror, garage door opener / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they followed the Owner's Manual to try and pair their gate, but the process did not work. Customer stated their previous 7 Series BMW had no issue pairing with the gate, but the system on this rearview mirror looks different and might not be compatible. 
Writer asked if, during pairing, customer was using the handheld remote or the button on the actual gate opener. 
Customer stated they were using the remote. 
Writer asked if the engine was running during the attempt. 
Customer confirmed the engine was on. 
Writer advised that putting in a fresh remote battery can help, since the system requires a strong signal to connect. 
Customer stated they are unable to do the second pairing method because it is a community gate and security will not allow it. 
Writer advised they could try again with the remote now. 
Writer advised to press buttons 1 and 3 until the indicator light changes frequency, explaining this clears any stored attempts. 
Writer advised then to hold the remote 1 to 3 inches from the rearview mirror, and press and hold the remote button together with the chosen mirror button until the light changes frequency. 
Customer stated the light turned green and began flashing. 
Writer asked if customer is able to test the gate with the mirror button. 
Customer stated the gate is currently more than 100 miles away and they will call back if needed.
