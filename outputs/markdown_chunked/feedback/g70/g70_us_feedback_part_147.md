# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-1964

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Back-up Assistant : Not user-friendly / Back-up Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that about 4 days after buying the vehicle he was using the Back Up Assistant to back out of his garage. Customer stated that when he lifted his foot off the brake pedal, the vehicle suddenly picked up speed on its own. Customer stated the car then made contact with the side wall of the garage and the driver side mirror was damaged. 
Customer stated his hands were not on the steering wheel at the time. Customer stated he had tried the Backup Assistant earlier in a parking lot and the same kind of speeding up occurred there as well. Customer stated that in the parking lot he did not hit anything. Customer stated it felt like the car sped up to roughly twice the speed it should have been going and that it was moving around 10-15 MPH. 
Customer stated the dealer has ordered the necessary parts. Customer stated that 5 of the 7 parts have arrived so far and he is still waiting for the remaining parts. 
Customer stated he has never used cruise control, so it was not switched on. 
Writer activated RVG and captured photos and video of the mirror, the area, and the steps the customer followed.
