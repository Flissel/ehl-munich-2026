### G70-2120

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Parking Assistant : Malfunction / Parking Assistant / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer explained he had stored a path into his garage for the maneuver assistant so that he can get out of the car and complete the parking process from his iPhone. Customer stated that ever since saving this route, the vehicle will notify him when he reaches the activation point, but the alert will not stop once it has started. Customer stated the gong keeps sounding repeatedly until he has driven roughly 2 miles and is well past the activation point. Writer asked if this occurs every time and customer confirmed it does. Customer also stated there is no way to remove any recorded paths from the menu. Writer asked if he can see the stored paths in the parking assistant, and customer said yes, but when he selects one there is no option to delete it. Writer asked whether the camera view stays on while driving or if only the alert continues. Customer stated that the display switches off once he reaches a certain speed and returns to the main iDrive screen, but the gong alert keeps going. Customer stated he has already updated apps and services in the vehicle and performed an iDrive reboot, yet the problem remains. Customer stated he intends to drop the vehicle off with service at the end of this week. Writer arranged a follow up call. Customer stated the vehicle has about 210 miles on it. Writer called again to check on the repair status, but the customer did not answer. No DCS or AIR Notes.
