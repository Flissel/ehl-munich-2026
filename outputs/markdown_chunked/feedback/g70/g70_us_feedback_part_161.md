### G70-2141

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Ambient light : Malfunction / Ambient light / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that when he sets the interior lighting to blue it stays that way for about two days and then changes back to clear. Customer stated he can switch it to blue again, but after another two days it reverts to clear once more. Writer asked follow-up questions to confirm the driver profile is functioning correctly. Customer stated his profile loads when he gets in, greets him by name, and does not switch over to the guest profile. Customer indicated his seat position remains the same and does not move. Customer stated he is the only person driving the car and uses just one key fob. Writer asked if any software updates had been performed. Customer replied he has not done any. Writer recommended he may need to go to his dealership so they can diagnose the concern.

There are currently about 1200 miles on the vehicle, according to the vehicle shadow.
