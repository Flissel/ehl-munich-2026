Customer comment:

> Customer stated he was unsure how the electric motor charges on longer drives and thought the battery would automatically fully recharge. Writer explained that only a small amount of charging happens during braking and coasting, giving just a few miles of range, and that a full charge requires plugging in. Customer stated he had seen that he might gain a couple of miles which then drop again and thanked the writer for explaining this is normal. Customer asked if it is okay to leave the car overnight without charging. Writer advised that this is fine, but noted the preconditioning feature will not operate without charge.
