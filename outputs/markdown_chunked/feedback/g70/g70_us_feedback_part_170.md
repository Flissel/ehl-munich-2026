### G70-2262

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Personal eSIM : Not user-friendly / Personal eSIM / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that a couple of times when he got into the car, the eSIM was either not connected or had no service, so his FireTV on the Theater Screen could not connect. Customer noted that when the eSIM failed to connect, he was in an area with poor cell service. Customer stated his Driver Profile was already signed in and he was using a Digital Key at the time. Customer added that once he arrived back home, he tried the eSIM again and it connected normally, and he was then able to use the Theater Screen with FireTV without issue. Customer confirmed the eSIM is currently still working normally.
Writer explained that the eSIM may have been impacted by the weak cellular reception. Writer mentioned the eSIM uses the car's antenna, which should improve reception compared to a phone, but it is still limited by areas with poor signal. Writer suggested checking for Remote Software Upgrades and for updates to Apps & Services in iDrive by going to the Apps Menu, then System Settings, then Remote Software Upgrade to look for available upgrades. Writer then advised checking for updates to Apps & Services under the same System Settings section. Customer stated he plans to check for these updates soon.

### G70-2265

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) : Not user-friendly / Automatic engine start-stop function (MSA) / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer asked about disabling MSA. Writer explained that in Sports Mode the vehicle is not in MSA. Writer advised to go in iDrive to "MENU" then "Vehicle apps" then "Driving settings" then "Drivetrain and chassis" then "Auto Start/Stop" and choose the wanted setting. Writer further advised to create a shortcut to the MSA screen by holding a finger on the display for about a second until the popup appears, then select yes to set the shortcut. Writer stated that to use the shortcut, place a finger at the top edge of the screen and drag down so the shortcuts become visible.
