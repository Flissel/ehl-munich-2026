### G70-2277

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Drive Recorder : Not user-friendly / Drive Recorder / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he is unsure how to turn on and operate the recording function, even after viewing instructional videos that show the vehicle recording scenery in natural areas. Writer explained that the customer should press and hold the button with the P symbol, waves, and camera icon on the center console for about one and a half seconds to start a recording, and that he can choose which cameras to use, including the overhead camera and the front camera. Writer confirmed each recording is roughly 30 seconds long. Customer then asked how the automatic recording feature can tell that an impact is about to happen before a crash. Writer advised that the system relies on the front sensors, mirror-mounted sensors, and rear bumper sensors to track the closing speed between the customer's vehicle and other vehicles, and when that closing speed suggests a possible collision, the system starts recording 20 seconds before the impact and continues for 30 seconds afterward.
