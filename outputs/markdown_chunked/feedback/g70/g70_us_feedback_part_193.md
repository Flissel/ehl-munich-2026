> i am both a bmw fan and a driving enthusiast. over roughly the last 50 years i have been lucky enough to own a variety of mercedes and bmw vehicles, including several s classes and several 7 series. i had a '22 760i that i really loved, and i originally ordered it with the autobahn package. bmw ended up deleting that package, but i still accepted delivery of the car. i already knew how good ars is because i had an '05 750i where ars was standard equipment, and that remains one of the best driving and riding cars i have ever owned. once bmw brought the autobahn package back, i felt i had to get it, and i have absolutely no regrets. the change in ride, handling, and steering response is very clear and noticeable, and this is honestly one of the best cars i have had. i just came back from a trip of a bit over 2000 miles and the car performed flawlessly. the way it combines ride comfort with handling is remarkable, with the suspension staying composed over all the different surfaces on i-95. i have taken that same drive many times in my other cars, and this one is the best "road" car so far. my only real comment is that i wish bmw would push acoustic control further. even though the car is already very quiet, i think adding true acoustic glass with an acoustic interlayer and proper labeling, together with active noise cancellation, could provide an extra 2-4db of noise reduction. i do not quite see why this has not been done yet, especially considering the rolls royce experience. maybe these features could be bundled into an "acoustic package". i am looking forward to the lci version expected in 2027. thank you, and please keep making great cars!
