### G70-2636

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Operate selector lever : Not user-friendly / Operate selector lever / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated this is his first BMW, and he is used to pushing the gear lever forward in his other cars to select Park and then getting out. Customer stated that in this vehicle, pushing the gear selector forward actually puts it into Reverse. Customer stated that if he then lets go of the brake pedal, the car begins to reverse. Writer stated this is something he should be aware of. Writer stated it may be helpful to turn on a feature that automatically activates the Parking Brake when the car is switched off. Writer stated to go into the Apps Menu, then choose Driving settings, Drivetrain and chassis, Parking brake, and switch on the option that engages the parking brake when the drive-ready state is turned off. Writer stated it is also a good habit to press the Parking Brake button after stopping the car. Writer stated that once this setting is on, either pressing the Engine Stop button or the Parking Brake button will place the car in Park and apply the Parking Brake.
