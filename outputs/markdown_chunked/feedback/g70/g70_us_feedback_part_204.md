- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE

- Signal types: complaint

- Defect (complaint): Flashing : Malfunction / Flashing / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when he makes a left turn, the turn signal does not shut off by itself, and he has to turn it off manually because it keeps blinking even when the steering wheel returns and he hears the click, but it still does not cancel. Writer asked if the customer plans to visit the dealer about the concern. Customer confirmed he will take care of it when the car is due for service or something similar, since he does not see it as an emergency issue. Writer confirmed he would record the issue and requested that the customer call back after having the turn signal inspected to let them know what the diagnosis was.
