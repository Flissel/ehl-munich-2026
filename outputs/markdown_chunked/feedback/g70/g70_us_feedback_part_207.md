Customer comment:

> Customer stated that when driving at highway speeds around 72 to 78 miles per hour the wind noise from outside always turns excessively loud. Customer stated it is the road wind doing this and believes it comes from the mirrors after talking with friends who own the same model. Customer stated this problem is not limited to their vehicle, as friends with the same model reported the exact same issues even after having the mirrors replaced or going to the dealer where technicians changed window seals and adjusted the door gaps. Customer stated they have not taken theirs in for this yet and are waiting for the G70 LCI to come out.
