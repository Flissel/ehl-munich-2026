### G70-2786

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): BMW ConnectedDrive upgrades/ MINI CONNECTED upgrades : Not user-friendly / BMW ConnectedDrive upgrades/ MINI CONNECTED upgrades / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he had received a notice about an alarm warranty with a thirteen day deadline and assumed the coverage had already lapsed. Customer said he was confused about whether he needed to do anything related to the stolen vehicle recovery alarm feature. Writer explained that the stolen vehicle recovery service uses GPS tracking if the car is stolen and is included at no cost for the first three years. Writer further clarified that there is no renewal or sign-up needed, as this service is automatically part of the new vehicle. Writer cautioned the Customer about possible scam letters or emails asking to renew warranties and confirmed that all warranties on the new car are valid and nothing must be renewed or subscribed to at this time. Writer also explained that ConnectedDrive services, which cover stolen vehicle recovery and roadside assistance, are active for the first three years and then change to a subscription model.
