### G70-2830

- Source: Source B

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Personal eSIM : Not user-friendly / Personal eSIM / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he would rather have the old way of getting wi-fi in the car instead of having to deal with esim setup. Customer stated the esim setup process was an absolute nightmare and that he put in more than around 20 hours trying to sort it out. Customer stated BMW and the carrier should have some kind of stop gap, because if a customer already has a device on their line using esim you cannot have another one. Customer stated that every time he started the vehicle, it triggered a request to his provider to authorize esim, and their system would run a credit check, approve the esim and add an extra line, then charge him. Customer stated that when he looked at his Verizon bill he noticed there were over roughly 24 esim lines on the account and he was being billed for each one. Customer stated he had to pay about a $1000 phone bill up front while working with his provider afterwards to remove all the extra lines and secure a refund. Customer stated that due to this situation he also found that his Apple Watch and his daughters Apple Watch no longer operate unless they are within range of their phones.
