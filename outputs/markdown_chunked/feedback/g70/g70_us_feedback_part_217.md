Customer comment:

> so far the car has been performing amazingly. it is very quiet, rides smooth, and is extremely comfortable. the auto drive / cruise control setup is good, but a bit confusing to switch between at first. i really wish there was clearer instruction and more direct control over the driving safety features. that stupid safety thing tries to brake constantly. and even when it needs to brake, why so hard and so early instead of more gradual? it makes me feel like i am driving badly and either irritating people by creeping toward stopped traffic or risking a wreak.

the touchscreens look great, but i would rather have more real buttons. it is awkward to tap the media "button" on the center console twice, you have to lift your finger fully and press again, which would be much simpler with a clicking button. all those controls could have been discrete buttons. there is no mute button on the steering wheel, which seems like a big oversight. and even when you do manage to hit the mute on the center armrest, it really just acts as pause - you can unmute but the player stays paused (at least on android with mediamonkey). i also don't get why the music doesn't actually pause when navigation gives directions; it just dips the volume like a radio, but a player could pause. my mercedes does both, with a steering wheel mute and pausing for instructions.

i also don't understand why the running lights stay on for hours; it seems tied to turning the car off then opening the door, or opening the door when the car goes to standby, but it's very confusing. the ac vents are useless if the car has been sitting in full sun, totally inadequate. i want finer control over a/c fan speed and vent routing - it's too easy for everything to get unsynced or for a small change to kick it out of auto mode. i wish the seat coolers were quieter. and i really wish there was an option without the glass roof, because that thing does nothing to block the sun and heat.

### G70-2899

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Vehicle, climatization : Not user-friendly / Vehicle, climatization / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he uses the Sync function to match the Heat and Air between the driver and passenger sides, but noted that every time he gets into the vehicle he has to press sync again. Writer asked if there was a passenger in the front seat. Customer replied that most of the time there is not. Writer explained that sync will only activate when a passenger is actually seated there. Customer then asked whether he could manage the rear seat climate settings. Writer confirmed he could, and stated via RVG to tap on Climate Control>Individual settings>second row of seats>settings. Customer also wanted to know how to adjust the Air Flow toward his upper body or his feet. Writer responded via RVG to tap Climate Control>the direction tab, and said to tap the airflow animations to switch the different zones on or off. Customer further inquired about the AUTO option. Writer stated that tapping AUTO will allow the system to automatically choose settings to best reach the desired temperature.
