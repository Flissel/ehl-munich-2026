# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-2904

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Rear seat entertainment : Not user-friendly / Rear seat entertainment / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he continues to pay for an eSIM, but the Amazon Fire TV on the Theater Screen has stopped working. Customer stated the eSIM is activated, connected to his BMW ID, and has been added as a device in the vehicle under the Mobile Devices menu. Customer stated that when he turns on the Theater Screen and selects the Amazon Fire TV input, the screen displays a prompt advising him to verify that the internet connection is active and that roaming is enabled. Customer stated he has previously watched an entire football game using Amazon Fire TV on the Theater Screen.
Writer stated to go to the Apps Menu, then Vehicle Apps, and then Mobile Devices to confirm that "Personal eSIM" appears. Writer stated to open the Personal eSIM settings to make sure the data connection is permitted and roaming is switched on. Customer stated he was inside the vehicle and verified these settings are correct. Writer stated to recheck that the Theater Screen source is set to Amazon Fire TV and that the customer Amazon account is properly linked in the settings. Customer confirmed both of those settings are correct. Customer stated he did not have additional time to continue troubleshooting and that he will consult the dealer regarding this concern. The writer was not able to obtain more details about the service provider or whether the data plan is unlimited.
