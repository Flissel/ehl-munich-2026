### G70-3055

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): My Modes / driving experience : Not user-friendly / My Modes / driving experience / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has been seeing some odd behavior with the iDrive. Customer stated that when he changes driving modes from his personal default mode to Sports Mode, cosmetic content disappears from the center display. Customer stated he has not yet figured out any specific sequence that triggers this, but he intends to try to track it later. Customer stated it is the cluster screen where the speedometer would normally appear, directly behind the steering wheel, and that the problem does not interfere with switching modes, only with the decorative graphics tied to each mode. Customer stated the car is running the latest iDrive update, and that this has occurred twice so far. Writer asked if the customer could be at the vehicle so the issue could be observed, but customer stated it is hard to recreate, and that both times it happened he was already driving.
