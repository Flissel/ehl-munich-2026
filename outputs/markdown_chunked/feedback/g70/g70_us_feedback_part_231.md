- Difficult to Use (complaint): Resting state after door opening : Not user-friendly / Resting state after door opening / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that during valet parking he leaves the key in the cupholder and closes the door, and every time he does this the vehicle begins beeping at him. Writer explained that when the system detects the key inside the vehicle and the door is closed, it will beep to alert that the key remains in the car. Writer advised the Customer to exit the vehicle with the key in his hand and then shut the door to avoid the beeping. Customer acknowledged.
