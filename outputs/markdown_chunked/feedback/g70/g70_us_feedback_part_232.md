### G70-3105

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Wants
- Signal types: unmet_need

Classifications:

- Wants (unmet_need): Ambient light : Customer request: usability / Ambient light / Customer request: usability

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that at night the ambient lighting creates a thick bright line that cuts right across the center of the driver side mirror, making it hard to see vehicles on the left side. Customer stated this happens every single night. Customer stated they tried using the night mode feature that automatically dims the ambient lighting, but even with it dimmed the light still shines through the mirror area. Customer stated they feel this is very dangerous because it obstructs visibility. Customer stated they attempted a workaround by wedging an envelope into the seam to block the light, but it keeps slipping out. Customer stated and asked if there is any way to program the system to turn off the ambient lighting just in that section on the left side. Customer stated and asked if the manufacturer could create something that could be placed in the seam and would stay there to block the light.
