### G70-3120

- Source: Source A
- Vehicle: G70

- Feedback types: Defect, Likes, Wants, unknown_feedback_type

- Defect (complaint): Bumper, rear : Damage / Bumper, rear / Damage
- Likes (praise): Drive, driving dynamics and chassis, overall technology : Like: function / Drive, driving dynamics and chassis, overall technology / Like: function
- Likes (praise): Electric Range : Like: Range / Electric Range / Like: Range
- Wants (unmet_need): Side window, open / close : Customer request: function / Side window, open / close / Customer request: function
- Defect (complaint): Trunk lid : Damage / Trunk lid / Damage
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> we absolutely love our i7. it rides so smoothly, looks really elegant, is packed with cool technology, and the real world range is quite good. we use it both for business and personal needs. we shuttle clients, mostly physicians at a clinic, and they are very impressed with the car and really like that it is an ev.

my spouse and i enjoy taking it everywhere. we have put close to 4800 miles on it in about 3 months, and it hardly feels like that much. it is a joy to drive on longer road trips.

there are a couple of things i would change. the subwoofer seems like it should be placed somewhere in the cabin. i think there is an upgraded diamond surround system that might add this. i owned a 2016 m5 before, and the bass in that car was just right. a friend has a 2024 ix, and i was amazed by the sound system in his.

ours is carbon black, so it gets washed often. one thing i noticed is that the rear trunk lid, especially the area under the rear bmw badge, traps a lot of water and it drains slowly. when it finally drains, it runs down onto the rear bumper and leaves water spots. we even use a blow dryer and it still causes some hassle. every car has its quirks, so i am not overly concerned.

also, i feel like the master window controls on the driver door should be moved back about 1-2 inches. even after 3 months, i still end up opening the rear windows when i mean to open the fronts. the placement feels unnatural. i'm 6 feet tall and have never really run into this in another vehicle.

overall, i really love this car and already look forward to owning another one someday. thanks for making such a gem of a vehicle.
