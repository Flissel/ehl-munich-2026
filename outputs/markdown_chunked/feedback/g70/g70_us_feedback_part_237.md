### G70-3166

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: PHEV
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Interior camera : Not user-friendly / Interior camera / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported not being able to use the interior camera to take pictures remotely through the BMW app and wanted to know the steps. Writer asked if the customer had acknowledged the camera in the vehicle and explained the privacy agreement must be accepted and the interior camera turned on in the car. Writer guided the customer at the vehicle to open the apps menu and go into the interior camera settings, then instructed the customer to open the BMW app and locate remote cameras under remote functions. Writer explained that privacy settings also need to be enabled in the Data Privacy menu, walking the customer through System Settings in the apps menu and then to Data Privacy, and outlined the service options including all services. Customer was able to find and understand the data privacy settings required for remote interior camera use. Writer advised in the BMW app to choose camera in the remote functions tab and select inside. Customer confirmed it works now.
