# G70 Feedback - Market: US

## Market: US

### G70-3168

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Air conditioning, setting : Not user-friendly / Air conditioning, setting / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated they like to have the air conditioning set to a cool temperature while running the seat heating with the massage on, and they cannot manage this easily in this vehicle without a lot of fiddling. Customer stated that in their prior 7 series this was very simple because the seat settings were not tied into the climate controls. Now they have to push the seat heat to a very high level to get it to come on, but then it is too hot, and in manual mode they find themselves constantly changing the fan and then the temperature, which is all too much for them. Customer stated this feels like a downgrade and a loss of a feature compared to their previous vehicle.
