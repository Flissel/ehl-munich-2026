### G70-3209

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Connection fault / Apple CarPlay / Connection fault

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the connection between the vehicle and Apple CarPlay is unreliable, as CarPlay will suddenly vanish and may or may not come back. Customer stated he is using an iPhone 15 Pro with current updates and, while it has fewer problems than his prior Android device, he still experiences these issues and occasionally must delete the phone profile and reconnect it. Customer stated that on his previous BMW he would hold the volume knob to reboot iDrive, but he was not able to do that on this BMW. Writer advised that pressing and holding the volume button beneath the iDrive controller for 30 seconds performs the same reset. Customer stated he was unaware of this and expressed his appreciation to the writer.
