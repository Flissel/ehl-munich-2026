### G70-3263

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Lane departure warning : Response characteristics: too jerky/too abrupt / Lane departure warning / Response characteristics: too jerky/too abrupt

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the Lane Departure Warning sensor feels extremely aggressive, to the point that at times he needs both hands on the wheel to counteract it. Customer reported that whenever he turns on the indicator and attempts to change lanes, the system still pulls him back into the original lane. Writer explained that the Steering Intervention should not be engaging while the turn signal is on and asked whether he moves over right away after signaling or waits briefly. Customer replied he has tried changing lanes both immediately and after a delay, and said on several occasions the system has nearly led to an accident, which has frightened him. Customer commented that he does appreciate the warning itself because it helps when he might be distracted, and noted he never drives with Cruise Control. Writer advised that the Lane Departure Warning vibration in the steering wheel can be adjusted and the Steering Intervention can be switched off. Customer mentioned he was not by the vehicle and refused a follow up call. Writer walked him through where to locate the settings, and Customer said he was relieved it can be disabled since he had been thinking about switching to another vehicle.
