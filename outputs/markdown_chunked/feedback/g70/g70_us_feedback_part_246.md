### G70-3272

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Apple CarPlay : Malfunction / Apple CarPlay / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that 2 days ago while using Apple CarPlay, the display froze on Navigation. Customer stated the lockup affected both his phone and the iDrive. Customer stated he turned the vehicle off and back on, but the situation remained unchanged. Customer stated the problem cleared by itself after about 2 hours. Customer stated he uses Apple Maps and that nothing else was impacted. Writer verified the phone (iPhone 12 MINI) and carrier (Spectrum), and asked if the iOS version was current. Customer stated he typically waits a few days before installing iOS updates. Writer suggested updating to iOS 17.0.3 and advised powering the phone down and turning it back on. Writer stated that once the phone restarts, customer should un-pair it, remove it from iDrive, and then pair it again.

### G70-3276

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): ConnectedDrive services (smartphone) technology overall : Not user-friendly / ConnectedDrive services (smartphone) technology overall / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that at random times the car speakers suddenly sound as if they are broken, with a crackling noise. Customer explained it will go on for around 30-40 minutes and then the sound returns to normal. Customer mentioned this happens when using CarPlay wirelessly, with CarPlay over USB, and also when only using Bluetooth. Customer described it as a high pitched squeal that makes music impossible to listen to, almost like the speakers are underwater. Customer stated the same thing happens on phone calls. Customer noted they have tried different iPhones: an iPhone 14 Pro, an iPhone Pro belonging to his wife, and a friend's iPhone 12. Customer added that the issue also affects the IPA. Customer said calls placed via the vehicle eSim are fine, and that Sirius XM and FM radio play with no problems. Customer reiterated that after about 30-40 minutes the sound is fine again, but it is so random that he has not been able to show it to the dealer. Writer asked if the phone is on the latest iOS, and customer confirmed it is not. Writer recommended updating to the current iOS, then fully powering the phone off and restarting it. Writer further advised un-pairing the phone, removing it from iDrive, and pairing again. Writer suggested rebooting iDrive by holding the radio power button for 30 seconds. Writer recommended a visit to the dealership and also suggested, when a passenger is present, attempting to capture a recording of the noise.
