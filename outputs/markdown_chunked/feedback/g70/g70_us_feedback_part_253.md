### G70-3364

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Lane Change Assistant : Not user-friendly / Lane Change Assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated his dealer advised that the car can switch lanes when the driver looks at the exterior rearview mirror. Writer confirmed this is a function called Automatic Lane Change Assistant. Writer explained that when Assisted Driving Mode is active, if the system senses adequate space in the next lane and the driver checks that lane by looking at the exterior mirror, the vehicle will carry out the lane change on its own. Writer added this can be disabled via Apps Menu, Vehicle, Driving settings, Driver Assistance, Driving, Assisted Driving, Automatic Lane Change Assistant.
