### G70-3379

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Intelligent Personal Assistant, activation word : Malfunction / Intelligent Personal Assistant, activation word / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer reports that their Wake Word function keeps shutting off. Customer noted that their daughter is a Genius at the dealership and they have already tried some troubleshooting together. Customer stated they completed a 30 second iDrive reboot. Writer accessed RVG and saw that the Wake Word activation slider was off, and when the customer attempted to switch it on, the slider turned green for a moment and then went back off. Customer was signed in with their BMW ID. Writer then performed a telecommunications reboot by holding the volume button for 70 seconds, allowing the iDrive display to power down three times, and afterward updated Apps and Services. Writer had the customer return to the Wake Word settings and the same problem persisted, with the feature still not able to be switched on. Writer apologized and recommended the customer schedule service. Customer agreed they would, but mentioned they are not sure when they will be able to go.
