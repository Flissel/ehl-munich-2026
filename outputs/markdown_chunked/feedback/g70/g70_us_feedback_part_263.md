Customer comment:

> Customer stated the booklet itself is quite small and that the print inside is very tiny, which makes it hard to read even while wearing eyeglasses. Customer stated he is close to 80 years old and does not have much patience for such small lettering. Customer stated he has had to make multiple trips back and forth to the dealer to get certain features set up because the manual is difficult for him to follow.
