### G70-3537

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Driver assistance, automated driving, overall technology : Malfunction / Driver assistance, automated driving, overall technology / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer stated that at times the Active Driving Assistant shuts off and tells him to take over, even though he has both hands on the steering wheel. Customer stated that when this happens several times in a row, the Active Driving Assistant becomes unavailable for a while. Customer stated he is confident his hands remain on the wheel and that no hands-on-wheel warning appears before the system stops. Customer stated this typically happens on highway drives and that the Driver Attention Camera in the Instrument Cluster is not blocked. Customer stated the car does not attempt to pull to the shoulder, it only instructs him to resume control, with a noticeable shake and slight slowing when it does so. Customer stated he has not contacted the dealer. Writer advised that if he feels the feature is malfunctioning, he should have the dealer diagnose it.
