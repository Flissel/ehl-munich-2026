### G70-3649

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Exterior lighting : Leak: water ingress/condensation / Exterior lighting / Leak: water ingress/condensation

Customer comment:

> Customer stated he has noticed water present in both front turn signal lights. Customer stated he does not expect to see water in the headlights on a car this expensive. Customer stated he has not contacted his dealer about it. Writer stated that the headlights are vented to the exterior so any water or condensation can escape through evaporation from the heat of the headlights. Writer stated that if this continues to be an issue, or if the customer notices pools of water forming, he should ask the dealer to further diagnose the issuse.

### G70-3652

Customer stated that at the bottom of the climate screen it shows her individual setting at a very low level, and then there is a small auto button, an AC indication, another auto button, and then a little button attached to auto that displays the defrost, head, and feet icons.
