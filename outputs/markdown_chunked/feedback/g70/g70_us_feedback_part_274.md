### G70-3673

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Highway assistant : Not user-friendly / Highway assistant / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated the dashboard interface is a bit challenging because the display screen sits far enough to the right that, when he looks at it in normal use, the driver distraction warning comes on in automated driving mode. Customer commented that the level five driving works very well overall, but the distraction monitoring seems overactive and frequently registers when he looks at the navigation or radio during regular driving. Customer noted he understands the idea of someone taking their eyes off the road for a long stretch, yet the system appears to trigger so quickly that it becomes difficult to even glance at the instruments. Customer suggested it likely would not trigger so much if the display were closer to the center or shown in the heads up display.
