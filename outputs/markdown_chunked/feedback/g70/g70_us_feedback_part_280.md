- Wants (unmet_need): Remote Software Upgrade/RSU : Customer request: function / Remote Software Upgrade/RSU / Customer request: function
- Wants (unmet_need): Space / spatial impression : Customer request: space / spatial impression / Space / spatial impression / Customer request: space / spatial impression
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> the auto doors are cool, but they really need to react quicker in terms of the delay before they move, and be more consistent. this clearly needs a software update.

the auto driving sensors are not fully dependable, especially once the weather is anything less than ideal.

in auto cruise, the automatic braking when coming up on slower or stopped traffic is not trustworthy at all; if you just let it go, it will basically plow into the other car even though it clearly knows the car is there. that is ridiculous and needs fixing.

with navigation, the voice will sometimes ask something like, "is this the final destination or an intermediate destination?" and when you answer with "intermediate destination" or something almost word-for-word from what it asked, it replies "i don't know what that means." that is obviously dumb.

auto park is too complicated just to have it locate and park in a simple spot, and yes, i do understand how to use it.

sport mode offers too few speedometer display choices.

ar guidance showing where to turn should have a green arrow on the road surface.

the way auto cruise decides how much to slow for corners is all over the place. it over-slows some bends and attacks others like an f1 car.

major problem: auto speed control won't reduce speed until after the posted limit changes and you press the set button. clearly the car should be able to adjust speed ahead of the sign, so there must be an option to anticipate upcoming lower limits.

i also need clearer visual feedback that the air suspension has raised the car and that i'm actually driving with it raised.

overall i'm pretty satisfied with it. i'd be happy to send more comments by email. i have several high-end vehicles, and this is my second 740.
