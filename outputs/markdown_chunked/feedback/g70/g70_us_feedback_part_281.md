### G70-3745

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Operating concept, operating system : Not user-friendly / Operating concept, operating system / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated valet parking was difficult because of having to activate the service card when using valet. Customer mentioned it requires two steps, first activating the service card and then using the valet setting, instead of a single option that would activate the card and handle the valet function together. Customer stated that when he checked valet parking information it showed valet mode for the key, which locks the trunk and glove box, but you still have to do those two things. Customer mentioned you need to do that so the valet does not steal from you, and then separately activate the service card so the valet can park the car. Customer stated that the first time he tried valet parking he was unable to use valet, and had to leave his phone because he did not realize he had to activate the card.
