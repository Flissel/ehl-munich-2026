### G70-3757

- Source: Source A
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE

- Signal types: complaint, praise, unknown

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Dynamic Stability Control : Malfunction / Dynamic Stability Control / Malfunction
- Likes (praise): Exterior design : Look / design appeals / Exterior design / Look / design appeals
- Defect (complaint): High voltage battery : Malfunction / High voltage battery / Malfunction
- Defect (complaint): Vehicle battery : Malfunction / Vehicle battery / Malfunction
- unknown_feedback_type (unknown): no_class_found / no_class_found

Customer comment:

> even though i really love the car itself, my first experiences with it have made me nervous about what owning it long-term will be like. the very first thing that went wrong was a dead 48 volt battery right at vehicle pickup, which meant i couldn't even drive my new $150,000 car home. i ended up waiting about a week while they sourced and installed a new battery.

then at just under 3,000 miles the dcs module failed, which made the brakes almost useless and nearly caused me to be part of a several-car pileup on a local freeway. this happened on december 23rd, during a drive from palm desert to pasadena (roughly 130 miles), and that made it even harder to get a tow truck out to me since i was about 88 miles from home. the wait for the tow was something like 3+ hours.

i've now been in a dealership loaner for over 10 days because the part my car needed was discontinued and replaced by a newer version that still isn't available. i will say bmw emergency service and my service advisors have been responsive, understanding, and sympathetic.

this is my 9th bmw, so i'm familiar with the quirks and issues they tend to develop as they get older, but seeing big problems like this on a brand-new vehicle feels pretty disheartening and discouraging. i waited a long time to be able to buy what i thought was the pinnacle of the bmw lineup, and now that i finally did, i feel somewhat let down.

that said, i truly love the look, the feel, and the way this thing drives; it has met or even exceeded what i expected from a flagship bmw. i'm hopeful that as we go forward my trust in bmw and its craftsmanship will come back. thank you for letting me share all of this.
