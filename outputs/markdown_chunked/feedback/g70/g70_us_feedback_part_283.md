Customer comment:

> Customer stated he was not certain how to set up the garage door opener and mentioned his garage door uses a rolling code. Writer instructed him to press and hold the chosen button on the interior mirror for about 20 seconds until the LED flashes orange. Writer then advised pressing and holding the button on the handheld garage remote close to the mirror, then pressing the "Sync" button on the garage door motor. Writer said to go back to the car and press and hold the same mirror button again until the LED lights up green.
