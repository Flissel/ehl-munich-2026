### G70-3903

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: ICE
- Feedback types: Defect
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Defect (complaint): Braking : Malfunction / Braking / Malfunction

Customer comment:

> Customer reported that ever since delivery he has needed to press down very firmly on the Brakes, putting the pedal all the way to the floor to bring the vehicle to a stop. Customer said he wanted the dealership to inspect this. Writer asked whether a similar issue was happening with the Accelerator pedal, and the customer replied that it was not. Writer then asked if the vehicle slowed or stopped when he applied the Brake. Customer stated the car would slow when he pressed the pedal, but would not completely stop, and reiterated that he had to press the pedal fully down to get it to stop. Customer mentioned they had taken the vehicle in for service roughly 2 days ago, and the dealership told him they did not find anything wrong with the Brakes at that time, but planned to test them further to make sure. Mileage around 1000.
