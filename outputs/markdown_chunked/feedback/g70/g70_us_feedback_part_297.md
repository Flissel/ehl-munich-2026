### G70-3978

- Source: Source B
- Vehicle: G70
- Market: US
- Engine type: BEVE
- Feedback types: Difficult to Use
- Signal types: complaint

Classifications:

- Difficult to Use (complaint): Wallbox, charging / home charging : Not user-friendly / Wallbox, charging / home charging / Not user-friendly

Customer comment:

> Customer stated one of the reasons he chose the i7 was the promotion that included a free BMW Wallbox along with a $2000 credit through Qmerit. Customer stated it has now been almost 2 months since he bought the vehicle and he still has no clear timing for when the BMW Wallbox will actually be installed. Customer stated Qmerit took roughly a month just to return his initial call. Customer stated Qmerit first tried to arrange an electrician based in Tampa, Florida, while he lives in Brookside, Florida, which is about 4 hours from Tampa. Customer stated Qmerit then scheduled a different electrician located in Laketon, Florida, roughly an hour and 20 minutes away from his home. Customer stated both of those electricians wanted to add extra charges because of the additional travel distance required. Customer stated they now finally have an electrician from Fairview, and Brookside is a suburb of Fairview, but the electrician advised it will be at least 3 more months before they can come out to install the Wallbox. 
Customer stated Qmerit is not living up to the expectations he has for a company associated with BMW. 
Customer asked to have his case escalated so BMW can look into a solution with Qmerit that might get his installation moved up, as he does not want to wait 6 months from the purchase date to have the Wallbox installed at his home.
